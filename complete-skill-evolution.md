```

    ↓

    ↓
```

## Architectural changes

1. **No new skills created** (137 → 137). Every gap was filled by upgrading existing skills; candidates for new skills (core-update-response) are documented, not invented.
2. **No skills deleted or merged.** The template discipline held; nothing needed consolidation.
3. **Cross-repo propagation** (per dependency graph): positioning research propagated into messaging (value-proposition, messaging-hierarchy, sales-deck); research synthesis propagated into CRO (message-match) and copy (customer language); paid attribution layer propagated into optimize (attribution-model-selection, crm-pipeline-attribution); SEO research propagated into messaging (content-strategy) and channels (programmatic-seo, serp-analysis); AEO research propagated into serp-analysis (AI-citation tracking).
4. **The meta-layer** (practitioner-intelligence) became the canonical evidence store: skills point at syntheses; syntheses point at dossiers; dossiers carry sources.
