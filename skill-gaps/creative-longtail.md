## Key encoded knowledge

1. **Creative supply rate is budget-scaled and the unit is the DISTINCT CONCEPT** — Denney (1–3/wk, cap ~10 at $5k–30k/mo) ↔ 2026 operator consensus (2–4 genuinely different concepts/wk at meaningful spend); "30 variations of one ad is one test" — Meta clusters near-identical creatives.
2. **Failed ads die at the hook** (Hormozi) — hook names buyer's pain in ≤4 words, 50 hooks before one ad, top 3 ship; layer visual+audio+text and caption-mirror for ~3x hold (social.md); thumb-stop 30–50% = good on Meta.
3. **Brief quality = output quality is the #1 UGC rule** — 5 independent sources agree (Moburst, ATTN +340%, InfluenceFlow 73%, SideShift, Pixis): 1–2 pages, angle + beats not scripts, red flags, revision + rights terms in the brief.
4. **Angle matrix before assets** — pains×proofs scored on evidence/saturation/proof; one angle → 20+ hooks; competitor ad-library scan is the saturation check.
5. **Ad fatigue ≠ creative fatigue** (AppsFlyer/Denney) — frequency + rotation for the former; supply + new angles for the latter; rotate every 7–10 days.
6. **Hook type must match Schwartz awareness level** — unaware→problem, problem-aware→pain callout, solution-aware→mechanism, most-aware→offer. Cold vs retargeting hooks are different jobs.
7. **UGC vs studio is conditional** — UGC wins cold/prospecting, studio wins retargeting/brand search; matched tests only; hook/angle/offer test order (offer last).
