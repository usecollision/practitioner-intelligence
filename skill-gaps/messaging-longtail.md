## Key encoded knowledge

1. **The hook window is 1.5-3 seconds** — 50-70% of video viewers leave in the first 1-2s; layered hooks (visual+audio+text) ~3x; core message in first 3s ≈ +60% total retention (Billo). New empirical grounding for video-scripts.
2. **Test creatives, not audiences** (Denney) — creative is the highest-leverage paid variable; budget-scaled testing structure encoded in ad-copy.
3. **Price objections usually mean value clarity** (offer-design rule) — now cross-referenced into objection-handling; discounting requires evidence it's a price problem.
4. **Create once, distribute forever** (Simmonds) — repurposing is distribution-first; resharing serves the 80% who missed the post; content-strategy now requires a distribution checklist.
5. **Earned insight + surprise** (Perell/Cole) — thought leadership is scar tissue, not summary; CRIBS test and room test encoded as gates.
6. **Transcreation > translation for emotional content** — lock the narrative frame, rewrite the punch line per market; literal translation inverts meaning at $10M scale (HSBC).
7. **Automation gated by quality thresholds** (Transifex TQI ≥0.95, Phrase conditional routing) — localization scales with TM+glossary+thresholds, human review for brand-critical content.
