## Key encoded knowledge

1. **"Facts from the past, not opinions about the future"** — the single most replicated rule in customer research (Fitzpatrick/Alvarez/Moesta/Revella/Christensen).
2. **Hypothetical questions are banned** — "would you buy" leads to any response; the milkshake feature-wishlist case failed exactly this way.
3. **Saturation, not quotas** — the count decision is now evidence-based per research goal.
4. **Persona fiction is the norm** — and AI personas trained on generic content are the same fiction at lower cost; AI allowed only on your transcripts.
5. **ICP from closed-won with quarterly refresh** — never firmographic wish-lists.
