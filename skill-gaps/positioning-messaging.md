## Key encoded knowledge

1. **Positioning is a choice against competitive alternatives** — the field's strongest consensus (Dunford/Pierri/Kellogg/Moore/Neumeier).
2. **Evidence precedes positioning**; AI cannot identify competitors — source shortlists from sales/customer data (Dunford).
3. **PMF precedes positioning** — most early "positioning problems" are fit problems (Shah, 40% very-disappointed gate).
4. **Category design is the exception, not the strategy** — Kellogg's 2026-08-13 critique encoded as the default skeptic.
5. **Market sophistication is copy's master variable** — direct claims fail in saturated markets (Schwartz); the OS now has a stage 1-5 diagnostic.
6. **Copy comes from research** — the strongest consensus in copywriting across 100 years (Wiebe/Ogilvy/Hopkins/Halbert/Price).
