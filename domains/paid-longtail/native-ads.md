# Panel — Native Ads (Taboola/Outbrain)

## Experts found
- **Marcel Sattler** (native-advertising.net; $100M+ deployed across Taboola/Outbrain/MGID/RevContent/etc. since 2015) — the deepest operator source: "If someone sold you on Taboola or Outbrain going green in three days, they lied" (T1).
- **Fabien Schwartz** (Schwartz Consulting, native ecom specialist) — CTR-is-not-profitability; S2S tracking; Blancheporte case −43% CPA / +100% ROAS (T2).
- **Joinative** — 9 native advertising mistakes; headline-content mismatch; last-click-only measurement blindness (T2).
- **OpenAdLibrary** — 12 headline formulas from live ad index (T3).
- **Taboola/Outbrain case libraries** — Kaspersky, Domino's (CPA 16% below target; CPC 347% more efficient than social), Zazume (−60% CPA retargeting), AVVA (+75% sales) (T2, platform-published).

## Beliefs
- **The CPA curve is INVERTED vs Facebook**: native CPAs start high and fall over 2–4 weeks of manual optimization, then stabilize; Meta starts cheap then explodes at scale (Sattler, EMPIRICAL — the single most important native insight).
- **You need cash flow to fund the convergence window**; starting native when liquidity is short is always a bad decision (Sattler, HEURISTIC).
- **Angle → Ad → Site three-layer optimization**, KPI-driven cuts only, never gut feeling; 3–5 editorials per angle, ~15 in test at once; CTR + editorial CTR are early signals only (Sattler, FRAMEWORK).
- **CTR is not a profitability metric** — app placements show high CTR/low CVR; judge CPA/ROAS on full funnel (Schwartz, CONSENSUS with Sattler).
- **Native feeds everything else**: clicks feed retargeting pools and other channels (halo) — Outbrain: increase in native traffic lifted conversions on other channels (Outbrain case; Schwartz; Joinative, CONSENSUS).
- Headlines: curiosity + unresolved verdict ("Tested: Does This $138 AC...") durable; headline must match content promise or bounce (OpenAdLibrary/Joinative).
- Bidding: don't trust Max-conversion bidding before data exists — budget burn for 1–2 leads (Schwartz).
- Tracking: S2S/server-side tracking is the key (Schwartz); last-click-only view makes native look ineffective (Joinative).

## Failure modes
- Expecting fast profit (3-day green) — the #1 beginner failure (Sattler).
- Headline/content mismatch — clickbait that doesn't deliver destroys behavior metrics (Joinative).
- Optimizing to CTR instead of CPA (Schwartz).
- No A/B testing (≥3 visuals, 2–3 landing pages) (Schwartz).
- Trusting platform auto-bidding without conversion data (Schwartz).
- Quitting before 2–4 week convergence (Sattler).
- Judging native on last-click alone (Joinative).

## Decision rules
1. IF running native THEN budget 2–4 weeks of runway at expected unprofitable CPAs before judging (Sattler, EMPIRICAL, T1).
2. IF CPA is high in week 1 THEN optimize angles/ads/sites — do NOT kill the campaign or cut budget (Sattler, EMPIRICAL, T1).
3. IF a native campaign is unprofitable after 4 weeks of structured testing THEN cut on KPI thresholds, never gut feel (Sattler, HEURISTIC, T1).
4. IF choosing KPI THEN use CPA/ROAS; treat CTR as an early diagnostic only (Schwartz/Sattler, CONSENSUS, T2).
5. IF launch day one THEN test ≥3 angles × 3–5 editorials × 2–3 landing pages in parallel (Sattler/Schwartz, FRAMEWORK, T2).
6. IF evaluating native's contribution THEN measure assisted/halo impact and feed clicks into retargeting, not just last-click (Joinative/Outbrain, HEURISTIC, T2).
7. IF cash-constrained THEN don't start native at all (Sattler, HEURISTIC, T1).
