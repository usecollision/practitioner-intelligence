# Panel — Spotify Ads

## Experts found
- **Orbis Agency** — bluntest statement found: "the strength of Spotify Ads is in brand recall and reach, not in direct click-to-purchase sales. If you expect an audio ad to generate immediate, trackable sales the way a Google search campaign would, you're going to be frustrated" (T2).
- **ATTN Agency** (growth/DTC) — audio CTRs 0.1–0.5% (lower than display but higher intent); cross-channel sequencing playbook; measurement = brand lift + search lift + geo incrementality (T2).
- **Esteban Largaespada** (Online Optimism, Digital Ads Director) — Spotify Advance 2025: Spotify Pixel, website-traffic objective, opt-in video ads (self-selecting engaged audience); display ads as low-risk entry before audio creative (T2).
- **Spotify Advertising docs** — Spotify Brand Lift (randomized test/control, in-app polls ≤48h post-exposure); first-party attribution; Koodo example +23pt ad recall (T1, platform).
- **R-Advertising** — targeted audio ads 24% higher brand recall than non-targeted (T3, cited study).

## Beliefs
- **The unit is CPM/audio impression + brand lift, NOT CPA** — the platform's own measurement stack is Brand Lift + reach/frequency + verification, not conversion ROAS (Spotify docs; Orbis; ATTN, CONSENSUS).
- High attention environment: 81% attention claim (T3 vendor stat); ads play in lean-back/headphone contexts.
- **The performance path exists but is indirect**: listeners → branded search → conversion elsewhere. So: run audio + raise branded-search bids during flight + retarget listeners visually (ATTN, FRAMEWORK).
- Attribution windows must be extended; audio converts later and cross-device (ATTN).
- Spotify is pushing into performance (Spotify Pixel, web objectives, conversion optimization) but operator consensus: it's still a brand/consideration channel (Largaespada; Orbis).
- Targeting: mood/activity/playlist contextual beats demographic (ATTN/R-Advertising).

## Failure modes
- Expecting direct-response CPA from audio ads ("the most misunderstood question") (Orbis).
- Overcomplicating 30-second creative — cramming (ATTN).
- No brand-lift or search-lift measurement → can't justify the channel (Orbis: "if an agency can't explain how it will measure that effect, that's your first warning sign").
- Judging CTR vs display benchmarks — structurally different (ATTN).
- Poor audio production burns budget without trace.

## Decision rules
1. IF the objective is direct-response ROAS THEN don't lead with Spotify audio; use it only as a brand/consideration layer (Orbis, OPINION, T2).
2. IF running Spotify THEN pair with: branded-search bid bump + visual retargeting of listeners during flight (ATTN, FRAMEWORK, T2).
3. IF measuring Spotify THEN require Spotify Brand Lift or search-lift (branded search volume) — dashboard CTR is not the verdict (Spotify docs/ATTN, FRAMEWORK, T2).
4. IF testing Spotify for the first time THEN start with display (low-risk) before producing audio creative (Largaespada, HEURISTIC, T2).
5. IF creative is >30s or multi-message THEN cut to one message (ATTN, HEURISTIC, T2).
6. IF budget < minimum viable for brand reach THEN skip — under-funded audio is untestable noise (synthesis of Orbis/ATTN, HEURISTIC, T3).
