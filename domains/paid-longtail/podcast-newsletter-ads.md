# Panel — Podcast & Newsletter Sponsorships

## Experts found
- **Terrence Ngu** (Hashmeta, 1,000+ brands) — host-read vs programmatic head-to-head with CPMs and attribution analysis (T2).
- **Ad Results Media** (podcast agency, via AdAge) — host-read persuasion mechanics: 45% of super listeners believe hosts actually use the products; non-host-read ads lag in performance; "just say both" (T2).
- **Springcast + Magellan AI** — 2026 CPM tables: host-read mid-roll $25–50 vs programmatic $15–25 (run-of-network $3–15); DAI now delivers 90%+ of podcast ad revenue (IAB) (T2).
- **Paved / SponsorGap / InfluencerFee** — newsletter CPM benchmarks and negotiation mechanics (T2–T3).
- **IAB** — podcast revenue report (T2).

## Beliefs
- **The unit is CPM on listeners/subscribers — and true cost should be lifetime**: host-read ads keep delivering for months/years in the back catalog, which lowers effective CPM (Hashmeta, HEURISTIC).
- **Host-read is a brand/trust buy**: 70–80% higher recall than programmatic; 45% of super listeners trust host endorsements; premium CPM ($25–80) for that trust (Hashmeta/Ad Results Media, EMPIRICAL).
- **Programmatic podcast = reach/targeting/scale at $15–35 CPM, RON $3–15** (Hashmeta/Springcast, EMPIRICAL).
- **Attribution is structurally weak**: promo codes are the standard but "require listeners to remember and enter codes manually — friction reduces measured conversions relative to actual influence"; vanity URLs better; branded-search monitoring + surveys + brand lift are the honest measurement (Hashmeta, CONSENSUS).
- **Hybrid wins**: host-read for flagship credibility + programmatic for efficient reach extension (Ad Results Media "just say both"; Hashmeta, CONSENSUS).
- **Newsletter economics** (SponsorGap/InfluencerFee/Paved): newsletter CPMs exceed display ($1–5), paid social ($5–15) and podcast ($20–50) because subscribers opted in; dedicated emails cost 2–3x inline; top placement +10–25%; category exclusivity +25–100%; **CPM is quoted on subscribers, not opens — smart buyers negotiate cost per open (≈2x effective)**; CTR >5% exceptional; finance/B2B niche CPMs highest (T2–T3).
- Measurement precision is newsletter's edge: opens/clicks/conversions trackable with UTM + promo codes (InfluencerFee, EMPIRICAL).
- High-trust editorial newsletters beat high-subscriber-count ones (InfluencersKit, OPINION/EMPIRICAL).

## Failure modes
- Measuring host-read on promo-code redemptions alone — massively undercounts (Hashmeta).
- Single-touch/last-click attribution for podcast — systematically undervalues the channel (Hashmeta).
- Buying newsletter slots on subscriber count instead of opens/CTR (InfluencerFee).
- No branded-search tracking during podcast flights (Hashmeta).
- Expecting geo/audience precision from host-read (limited) (Hashmeta).
- Cold-buying without vetting the host's genuine usage/credibility (Ad Results Media trust mechanics).

## Decision rules
1. IF objective is trust/consideration for complex or premium products THEN buy host-read on niche shows (CPM $25–80) (Hashmeta, HEURISTIC, T2).
2. IF objective is reach/efficiency for simple impulse products THEN buy programmatic podcast (RON $3–15; targeted $15–35) (Hashmeta/Springcast, EMPIRICAL, T2).
3. IF measuring a podcast flight THEN run promo code + vanity URL + branded-search volume + new-customer survey in parallel — never promo codes alone (Hashmeta, FRAMEWORK, T2).
4. IF negotiating newsletters THEN price on opens and CTR, not subscribers; require open-rate data; use UTM + promo codes (InfluencerFee/SponsorGap, HEURISTIC, T2).
5. IF renewing a sponsorship THEN require click/conversion evidence (UTMs, promo redemptions) before premium rates (InfluencerFee, HEURISTIC, T2).
6. IF budget is small THEN one high-fit host-read show beats five programmatic sprinkles (Ad Results Media/Hashmeta, OPINION, T2).
