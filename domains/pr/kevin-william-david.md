- ## Beliefs — Product Hunt is a community game, not a voting game: the audience is empathetic and patient with founders (pile-ons almost never happen), feedback is measured and qualitative, and the community actively polices bots/ballot rigging. The 24 hours after launch are the game.
- ## Principles — Preparation beats luck: reach out to top hunters 2-3 weeks ahead; pick launch day by audience (B2B/enterprise/work tools → Mon-Thu, when people are working and attentive; funded companies cluster Tue/Wed); avoid megacorp announcement days unless you commit to out-shouting them (e.g., launch against WWDC if you believe in the product); understand the ambition of the listing (MVP launches can disappoint — launch what you're proud of).
- ## Frameworks — "Bullseye" launch: day selection → hunter relations → listing assets (tagline, description, images, demo) → 24h engagement → post-launch follow-up (community + email to voters).
- ## Processes — 2-3 weeks out: contact hunter + prep assets; launch day: go live early, engage every comment for 24h, respond to feedback, thank voters; post-launch: convert momentum (email list, product demos, community invites).
- ## Heuristics — if you can't staff 24h comment engagement, reschedule the launch; if your product is an MVP you're embarrassed by, fix it before PH (community is kind but remembers).
- ## Metrics — day-1 upvotes, comments depth (feedback quality), rank by category, signups attributed to launch, email capture, follow-up activation.
- ## Decision rules
  - IF B2B/enterprise product THEN launch Mon-Thu (Tue/Wed best for work attention). (HEURISTIC, T2)
  - IF a major player announces the same day THEN either avoid or fully commit — no half-measures. (HEURISTIC, T2)
  - IF no audience to bring on day 1 THEN build pre-launch community/email list before launching. (HEURISTIC, T2)
  - IF you can't engage comments for the full 24h THEN move the launch date. (HEURISTIC, T2)
  - IF you want a top hunter THEN contact 2-3 weeks ahead — calendars fill 1-2 weeks out. (HEURISTIC, T1 from his own page)
- ## Failure modes — botting/ballot rigging (community-police it and it backfires); MVP embarrassment; launching on a dead day (Fri/weekend for B2B); ghosting the comment section; no post-launch follow-up.
- ## Conditions — B2B SaaS and consumer tools; works best with an existing audience + launch-ready product; PH value is concentrated on launch day.
- ## Limitations — T2: his guidance is interview/advice-based, not controlled data; Chris Messina's launch-culture essays not fetched this session (T3); PH mechanics shift (algorithm, "featured" changes).
