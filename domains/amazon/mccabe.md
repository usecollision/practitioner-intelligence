# Chris McCabe — Amazon enforcement, account health & compliance

## Beliefs
- Sellers must "think like Amazon": understand how Amazon's policy teams evaluate accounts and make reinstatement decisions (ecommerceChris; Jungle Scout contributor bio).
- Proactive compliance beats reactive appeals — most suspensions are avoidable with an audit of every customer-facing touchpoint (AM/PM Podcast #379, 2024).
- Escalation and communication are skills: sellers who document and communicate well get faster, better outcomes than those who fire off angry appeals (AM/PM #379).

## Principles
- Amazon's enforcement is broader than sellers assume; "innocent" practices (packaging inserts asking for reviews) trigger suspensions (consistent with SellerSprite/Cabilly 2026 cases).
- Your account health is a business asset — manage seller metrics, listing compliance, and communications like operations, not legal afterthoughts.
- Third parties (manufacturers, agencies) can get you suspended without your knowledge; audit them (SellerSprite cases; McCabe's client work).

## Frameworks
- **Appeal/POA (Plan of Action) discipline**: root-cause analysis → corrective actions → prevention; document proportionality (sales data, campaign records) when enforcement hits legitimate growth (AM/PM #379 + SellerSprite velocity guidance consistent with McCabe's teaching).
- **Think like Amazon**: model what Amazon's investigator sees (metrics, policy text, patterns) before writing anything.

## Processes
1. Full account audit: seller metrics, listing compliance, packaging/communications, supply chain docs.
2. Diagnose suspension type and severity (policy vs performance; review-related vs IP-related).
3. Build the appeal with root cause + corrective + preventive actions; escalate through the right channels.
4. Post-reinstatement: compliance monitoring to prevent recurrence.

## Heuristics
- Never offer anything of value in exchange for a review (incentives = hardest to appeal).
- If review velocity spikes, prepare the proportionality story (sales + marketing records) *before* Amazon asks.
- Manufacturer-added inserts still count against you — control your packaging spec.
- One documented misstep can now suspend a clean account; enforcement tolerance collapsed post-2023 (Cabilly/McCabe-adjacent 2026 commentary).

## Tactics
- Escalation via seller support hierarchies and account-health representatives.
- Brand Registry for abuse reporting and listing control (McCabe teaches reporting abuse).
- Pre-launch compliance reviews for new products/campaigns.

## Tools
- Seller Central (performance dashboard), Brand Registry, appeal channels, ecommerceChris team (incl. Leah McHugh, listing compliance expert).

## Inputs
- Suspension/performance notifications, seller metrics, listing history, supplier/packaging documentation, marketing records.

## Outputs
- Reinstatement appeals, compliance audits, escalation strategies, prevention playbooks.

## Metrics
- Reinstatement time (industry: ~23 days for false-flag insert cases), account health score, seller metrics (ODR, late shipment, cancellation).

## Decision rules
- IF suspension involves review manipulation THEN prove proportionality/documentation first; never deny without evidence (HEURISTIC, T2).
- IF packaging inserts exist anywhere in your supply chain THEN remove them or document lack of control before launch (HEURISTIC, T2).
- IF a third party (agency/manufacturer) touches customers or listings THEN audit their practices for compliance (HEURISTIC, T2).
- IF account has a policy warning THEN treat as pre-suspension; remediate before scaling ads (HEURISTIC, T2).
- IF you lack documentation for a velocity flag THEN gather sales + campaign records before appealing (HEURISTIC, T2).

## Failure modes
- Buying fake reviews / review swaps — instant suspension, funds frozen (SellerSprite: $28k frozen case; McCabe interview: sellers paying $10k/month for fake reviews get caught).
- Asking customers to revise/remove reviews for compensation.
- Ignoring account-health notifications while scaling PPC — ads amplify a dying account.
- Appealing without root-cause analysis (template appeals fail).

## Contrarian beliefs
- Enforcement is *not* fair or precise: legitimate launches get flagged for velocity; manufacturer materials count against you (2026 SellerSprite cases align with McCabe's warnings).
- Don't try to game Amazon; treat policy as the operating system, not an obstacle.

## Conditions
- His method matters most for accounts at risk (new sellers, high-velocity launches, resellers, brands with complex supply chains); low-risk stable accounts need only the audit.
