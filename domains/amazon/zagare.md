# Mike Zagare — Amazon PPC education & campaign mechanics

## Beliefs
- A well-educated seller is a successful seller; Amazon PPC is learnable and systematizable, not black magic (PPC Entourage mission).
- Amazon advertising should engage shoppers at every stage of the Customer Buying Cycle — budget allocation follows the buyer's psychology, not just the auction (ZonGuru guest article, 2020).
- Starting bids should be set from data (suggested bids, category CPCs, margins), not guesses (PPC Entourage blog, 2020).

## Principles
- Keyword relevance first: harvest from auto/broad, promote proven terms, negate junk (Blueprint Series process).
- Rank campaigns by search volume: high-volume keywords get their own campaign (single-keyword rank campaigns); group 5–10 mid-volume; up to 10–15 max per campaign (ZonGuru video transcript, 2021).
- If you're going for rank, use exact match and fight for Top of Search; combine match types for maximum reach (ZonGuru transcript).
- Cross-check keyword tools against Amazon Brand Analytics — the tool should find keywords BA misses (ZonGuru transcript).

## Frameworks
- **6 Options for Starting Bids** (PPC Entourage, 2020): (1) Amazon suggested bid — start at low end of bid range and inch up; new products need higher bids; (2) product average CPC — bid at/above category avg CPC for visibility; (3) average max CPC; (4-6) margin/objective-based methods (profit-margin-derived bids, goal-ACoS-derived bids, competitor-informed). HEURISTIC.
- **Customer Buying Cycle budgeting** (ZonGuru, 2020): awareness-stage spend builds brand halo; allocation across funnel stages grows long-term share.

## Processes
1. Keyword research ≤ 20 minutes with a tool + Brand Analytics cross-check (ZonGuru demo).
2. Filter to high-volume, high-relevance terms; check competitor positions (top-25 competitor rank, units/day to beat).
3. Estimate spend-to-rank math: clicks-per-sale × CPC = cost per sale (e.g., 7 clicks × $1.50 = $10/sale) — he admits the math is rough ("I actually think this math is a little off"), use as directional.
4. Build campaigns by volume tier; set bids from the 6-option framework; weekly harvest loop.

## Heuristics
- Suggested bid range is category-level; new products must bid higher than the range midpoint.
- Terms with high impressions but no clicks = relevance waste → negate.
- Click-to-order ratios (~7 clicks/sale) and avg CPC (~$1.50) are starting assumptions, never constants.
- Don't harvest a term you haven't seen convert.

## Tactics
- Single-keyword campaigns for top-5 high-volume terms; keyword grouping by volume tier.
- Exact-match Top-of-Search play for rank goals; broad/phrase layered for reach.
- Weekly search-term report rhythm with promote/negate decisions.

## Tools
- PPC Entourage (agency), ZonGuru (Keywords on Fire — the demo tool in the interview), Brand Analytics.

## Inputs
- Search volume, competitor rank/units data, Brand Analytics keywords, CPC benchmarks, margin data.

## Outputs
- Campaign structures, bid strategies, budget estimates, keyword harvest plans.

## Metrics
- ACoS, CPC, clicks-to-sale ratio, rank position, search-term conversion.

## Decision rules
- IF new product THEN bid above suggested range; run auto + broad to harvest (HEURISTIC, T2).
- IF high search volume term THEN own campaign, exact match, Top-of-Search focus (HEURISTIC, T2).
- IF term has clicks but no sales past threshold THEN negate (HEURISTIC, T2).
- IF term has impressions but no clicks THEN negate as relevance waste (HEURISTIC, T2).
- IF goal is rank THEN tolerate break-even+ ACoS during launch window (HEURISTIC, T2).

## Failure modes
- Optimizing every keyword to the same ACoS regardless of stage.
- Starting bids blind (no suggested-bid/CPC reference).
- Letting auto campaigns run without harvest/negate discipline.
- Scaling spend on listings that don't convert.

## Contrarian beliefs
- Budget allocation should follow the customer buying cycle (awareness→consideration→purchase), not just ROAS — an early (2020) voice for halo/brand thinking inside pure-play PPC.
