## Beliefs
- "There is no single AI Visibility": ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews are three different distribution systems that mostly don't overlap. — EMPIRICAL (his 3.7M-citation study, May 2026)
- A blended/composite AEO score is the wrong unit of measurement: averaged scores hide single-engine dependence. "A brand can look strong in aggregate and be invisible in 2 of 3 engines." — EMPIRICAL + OPINION
- Citation frequency ≠ citation portability: volume and universality are different properties. — EMPIRICAL
- AI engines are converging only slowly: universal overlap rose from 2.2% (Q3 2025) to 2.7% (Q1 2026); engine-exclusive citations fell from ~90.1% to ~88%. Fragmentation still dominates. — EMPIRICAL (longitudinal)
- Low portability is often an "entity definition problem": the brand is too diffuse for three retrieval systems to pick the same facet. (echoed in his LinkedIn thread) — OPINION/HYPOTHESIS

## Principles
- Measure AI visibility as three separate numbers: Presence, Portability, Concentration.
- Never compress engines into one leaderboard.
- Distinguish "cited a lot" from "cited everywhere."

## Frameworks
- Presence / Portability / Concentration (his core measurement framework, May 2026):
  - Presence = share of tracked prompts where your domain appears in ANY engine.
  - Portability = share of your cited URLs that appear in all three engines.
  - Concentration = share of your citations coming from one engine (high concentration = visibility sits on one platform's preferences).
- The Consensus Gap concept: engines draw from largely disjoint pools (91% single-engine) rather than ranking the same pool differently.

## Processes
- Run recurring citation audits across engines (he has run multiple since 2024, e.g., AI Overviews vs ChatGPT brand-visibility comparisons), now at 20k prompts / 3.7M citations scale using Omnia data. — PROCESS
- Report per-engine presence + overlap analysis; track universal-overlap rate over quarters.

## Heuristics
- Guides and tutorials travel across engines ~2x better than homepages (from his 2026 study commentary). — EMPIRICAL
- If a domain is dominant in aggregate but concentrated in one engine, the aggregate number is hiding the risk.
- Wikipedia and Reddit get cited massively but are almost never universal (Wikipedia 16,073 appearances, 1.3% universal; Reddit 14,267, 0.1% universal; Reuters 1,202, 0.0% universal) — a lesson in volume vs portability.

## Tactics
- Track presence, portability, and concentration per engine per prompt library.
- Watch portability as the strategic metric: it reframes the question from "am I optimized for AI?" to "have I made myself unambiguous enough that three different retrieval systems agree I'm the right answer?"

## Tools
- Omnia (citation dataset); Growth Memo's own tracking stack; AI engines themselves (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews/AI Mode).

## Inputs
- Large prompt samples (his: 20k prompts); citation-level data across engines; longitudinal samples per quarter.

## Outputs
- Growth Memo studies ("The Consensus Gap," May 2026); recurring AI search visibility reports; consulting frameworks.

## Metrics
- Presence %, portability %, concentration %, universal-overlap rate over time, engine-exclusive citation share.

## Decision rules
- If a brand's citations are >~80-90% from one engine → treat AI visibility as single-platform risk; do portability work (entity clarity, third-party footprint) before scaling content.
- If universal overlap across your vertical is low (typical) → optimize per engine where the buyer actually asks, don't chase one universal ranking.
- If a blended score says "strong" but presence in your key engine is weak → trust the per-engine number.
- When tracking, use the same prompt set across engines; treat each engine's answer as a sample, not a ranking.

## Failure modes
- Composite AI-visibility scores that mask concentration (his named enemy).
- Confusing citation volume with portability (Wikipedia/Reddit trap).
- Optimizing for one engine's preferences and calling it "AI visibility."

## Contrarian beliefs
- Most AI-visibility tools' headline "AI visibility score" is misleading by construction.
- Even the most-cited platforms (Wikipedia, Reddit) are poor ports between engines — so "get cited on Reddit" advice has a ceiling for cross-engine reach.

## Examples
- The Consensus Gap study: 3.7M citations, 20k prompts, ChatGPT+Perplexity+AIO; 2.37% universal, 91.07% single-engine.
- Longitudinal overlap data (2.2% → 2.7% universal across Q3 2025–Q1 2026).

## Conditions
- His framework needs prompt-level citation data (Omnia-grade); for teams without it, start with a manual 50-100 prompt sample per engine.
- Portability matters most for brands whose buyers use multiple engines; single-engine verticals (e.g., AIO-heavy local search) can optimize presence-first.

## Limitations
- Observational (no control group); Omnia data is a convenience sample of prompts.
- Portability guidance for *local* businesses is still forming (his thread notes local brands' training-data problem without a full solution).
