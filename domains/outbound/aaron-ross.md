## Beliefs
- Outbound is a repeatable system (Cold Calling 2.0), not charisma; dedicated prospectors + scripts + quota = predictability (FRAMEWORK).
- Email conversion has decayed structurally: ~7% reply-to-call in 2010s → ~0.7% today; the bar for engagement is much higher (EMPIRICAL — his company's data).
- "Send more email!" is the new knee-jerk reaction replacing "make more calls!" — doing more of what's not working isn't a solution (PRINCIPLE).
- Prospectors must master 2–4 complementary techniques, not over-rely on one (PRINCIPLE).

## Principles
- Separate roles: prospector (finds + qualifies) vs closer (demo/close); cold calling 2.0 separates "hunters" from "farmers" (FRAMEWORK).
- Pick up the phone — live conversations every day (PRINCIPLE).
- Human error grows with automation: more activity = more errors in response handling and follow-up (PRINCIPLE).
- Data is a never-ending problem; prospectors spend 2–3 hours/day building/fixing lists (PRINCIPLE).

## Frameworks
- Cold Calling 2.0: targeted email + phone sequences by dedicated SDRs with scripts, quotas, and metrics (FRAMEWORK).
- Outbound Validation / Outbound Labs: run up to 20 experiments/month per client — 3–7 target segments × 3–7 message options; only 2–3 hypotheses win, ~15 fail; classify ALL responses for learning (FRAMEWORK).
- Jobs-to-be-done targeting: target people by the job they must execute (not title): goal → jobs → obstacle (FRAMEWORK).
- AWAF ("Are We A Fit") calls: prospector holds initial fit call, then books demo/discovery with senior closer (FRAMEWORK).

## Processes
- Identify JTBD-based targets → craft message hypotheses → test 20 experiments/month in email (cheap medium) → classify every response (positive/neutral/negative) → feed learning into next round (PROCESS).
- Prospector AWAF call (15/week vs industry ~16/month) → pass to closer → close (PROCESS).

## Heuristics
- Email is now cheap enough to be the experimentation medium, even as conversion drops (HEURISTIC).
- Dashboard/funnel metrics are usually wrong: human error + config errors make executives blind (HEURISTIC).

## Tactics
- Targeted list of people "who have to get the same job done" rather than title lists (TACTIC).
- Validate targets and messages before scaling spend (TACTIC).

## Tools
- Carburetor (orCarb.io) pipeline automation; Salesforce (his origin context) (TOOLS).

## Inputs
- JTBD map of the market, hypothesis matrix (targets × messages), response classification taxonomy (INPUTS).

## Outputs
- Predictable outbound machine: SDR org design, scripts, quotas, experiment pipeline, metrics dashboards (OUTPUTS).

## Metrics
- AWAF call quantity per prospector (16/month → 15/week = +400%), AWAF call-to-close rate (4.6% → 12%), email reply-to-meeting conversion (~7% → 0.7% decay), experiments won/lost per month (METRICS).

## Decision rules
- If a technique's conversion decays → treat it as an experiment input, not a religion; revalidate (DECISION RULE).
- If 20 experiments yield 2–3 winners → scale winners, kill the rest, keep classifying flops for learning (DECISION RULE).
- If metrics can't be trusted → fix tracking before optimizing (DECISION RULE).

## Failure modes
- Copying Predictable Revenue tactics (SDR teams, cadences, meeting quotas) onto systems not designed for them — "most companies failed" at copying it (FAILURE).
- Overdependence on a single technique (research OR cold calling OR templates) (FAILURE).
- Automation increasing human error in response handling/follow-up (FAILURE).
- Task/tool/app overwhelm from high-volume prospecting (FAILURE).

## Contrarian beliefs
- Cold calling 2.0's original email approach decayed ~10x; the discipline needs validation-first experimentation, not more volume (OPINION).

## Examples
- Salesforce: dedicated prospectors + Cold Calling 2.0 → predictable pipeline; call-to-close tripled with better data/targeting (EXAMPLE).
- Client program: AWAF calls 16/month → 15/week per prospector; call-to-close 4.6% → 12% (EXAMPLE, his company data).

## Conditions
- B2B SaaS/enterprise with defined ICP and JTBD clarity; orgs with separate SDR/closer capacity; needs measurement infrastructure (CONDITIONS).

## Limitations
- The full machine (SDR team, quota system, experiment ops) is expensive; solo/SMB operators can't run 20 experiments/month without list + tooling budget (LIMITATIONS).
