## Beliefs
- "There is no cold calling in the Lion Program" — call it warm calling: research first, then call people you've established some context with; you're calling to help, not to qualify (FRAMEWORK).
- You haven't earned the right to qualify on a connect call — get the other person talking (PRINCIPLE).
- "If you are not researching, you don't deserve the person on the other end to pick up the phone" (PRINCIPLE).

## Principles
- Respect: you are an executive speaking to an executive; one human being to another (PRINCIPLE).
- Be helpful even if they never buy — helping is the competitive advantage (PRINCIPLE).
- Gatekeepers are "gate openers": treat them with respect, learn how the office works (voicemail/email routing) (HEURISTIC).
- Number one prospecting tip: pick up the phone; call 4 times with intermittent emailing (HEURISTIC).

## Frameworks
- Lion Program: sales training for HubSpot partners (~800 agencies/yr) built on motivation + warm calling + help-first mindset (FRAMEWORK).

## Processes
- Research prospect → warm call (help frame, no qualification) → gather information & build rapport → follow up with video email → call again (up to 4 calls) (PROCESS).

## Heuristics
- Video in sales: cold call converts ~1-2%, inbound follow-up ~12-15%, process with video ~30-40%; only ~25% of reps use video (EMPIRICAL — his cited stats, unverified independently).
- Ask the gatekeeper how the decision-maker prefers contact (voicemail vs email) — transformational context (TACTIC).

## Tactics
- Personalized video (simple Soapbox-style intro) to warm the coldest channel — email (TACTIC).
- On connect calls: focus on positive, be helpful, provide context; never start with your agenda (TACTIC).
- Motivation as infrastructure: maintain energy via training programs (TACTIC).

## Tools
- HubSpot CRM (automation of tracking, notifications on opens/video views), video tools (Soapbox-style) (TOOLS).

## Inputs
- Research on prospect and company before any call; info on gatekeeper/office routing (INPUTS).

## Outputs
- Warm-call scripts, partner sales training (Lion Program), motivation systems (OUTPUTS).

## Metrics
- Call/meeting conversion rates, video engagement (views), partner business growth (METRICS).

## Decision rules
- If you haven't researched → don't call yet (DECISION RULE).
- If cold emailing → include a video or don't expect warmth (DECISION RULE).
- If blocked by gatekeeper → build relationship with gatekeeper, learn routing, keep the "I'll call again soon + I want to help" frame (DECISION RULE).

## Failure modes
- Cold calling = same script to strangers: "it hurts your brand and your business" (FAILURE).
- Qualifying on the connect call before earning the right (FAILURE).
- Starting with your agenda/company story instead of their context (FAILURE).

## Contrarian beliefs
- Phone is not dead — it's a pillar; 4 calls + intermittent email is the base cadence (OPINION).

## Examples
- Lion Program: ~800 agencies participating, most see nearly double business YoY (EXAMPLE, HubSpot partner data).
- Video-in-process conversion uplift 1-2% → 30-40% (his cited numbers; treat as directional) (EXAMPLE).

## Conditions
- SMB/partner-sales context with reachable decision-makers; markets where phone + video are culturally acceptable (CONDITIONS).

## Limitations
- Phone-first warm calling doesn't scale to 10k+ prospect universes; video production per prospect is expensive; stats are self-cited (LIMITATIONS).
