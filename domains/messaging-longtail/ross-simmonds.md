- ## Beliefs — "Create once. Distribute forever." (his book's thesis). Most content dies in obscurity not because it's bad but because nobody sees it. "Most people stop at hitting publish... the work happens after you press publish." Content strategy is backwards: teams spend too long creating and almost no time distributing. Disney's playbook (remake, repurpose, reinvent for new audiences/formats) is the model for modern brands.
- ## Principles
  - The Three Rs: Repurpose (same idea, new formats: webinar → blog post, newsletter, eBook), Remix (same content, new consumption experience: audio to Spotify, best 5-minute segment as a social clip), Reshare (the most underutilized — only ~20% of your audience saw the post the first time; resharing serves the 80% who missed it; a repeat viewer says "thanks for the reminder," not "unfollow").
  - Four E's framework for content worth distributing: Educate, Engage, Entertain, Empower. Content should fall into one of these to be worth the distribution effort.
  - The media engine has 5 components: Creation, Optimization, Budget, Distribution, Research — distribution is a named pillar, not an afterthought.
  - Channel-specific, not copy-paste: "You have to understand the voice and tone of the communities you're going into, and articulate and mirror that style" (Reddit rule: mirror tone, be transparent with numbers/screenshots, give value back to the community).
  - AI as a high-paid consultant, not a content replacement: upload brand voice docs and ask AI to find gaps; use it to improve strategy, not to do the creative work.
  - Not all assets are created equal: distribute the hits and evergreen assets; don't spread mediocre content everywhere.
- ## Decision rules
  - IF only ~20% of the audience saw a post THEN resharing is service, not spam (Simmonds — EMPIRICAL/T2).
  - IF an asset is evergreen THEN run it through repurpose + remix + reshare; IF not, don't (Simmonds — FRAMEWORK, T1).
  - IF entering a community (Reddit, Quora, groups) THEN mirror its voice, be transparent, and give value before promoting (Simmonds — HEURISTIC, T1).
  - IF distribution is an afterthought in the plan THEN the plan is incomplete — budget and channel research belong next to creation (Simmonds — FRAMEWORK, T1).
  - IF the audience lives on a specific channel THEN build the repurposing strategy for that channel's native formats (carousels for LinkedIn, reels for Instagram) (Simmonds — HEURISTIC, T1).
- ## Metrics — reach per channel, engagement, pipeline/leads from distributed content ("Millions of visits. Leads that convert. Pipelines that close."); he rejects vanity metrics.
- ## Failure modes — stopping at publish; one-and-done posting; copy-pasting the same post to every channel; distributing mediocre content; ignoring communities where the audience actually is; treating AI as an author instead of a strategist.
- ## Conditions — B2B and SaaS content engines; brands with recurring evergreen assets; teams with distribution capacity; works for small teams via the Three Rs on one strong asset.
- ## Limitations — his agency sells distribution services (incentive bias); ~20% reach figure is a rule-of-thumb from platform behavior, not a published study; channel specifics shift with platform algorithms.
