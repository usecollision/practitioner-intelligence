- ## Beliefs — Creative is the highest-leverage variable in paid social; test creatives, not audiences. Ad accounts die from creative fatigue, not targeting. Messaging learnings come cheap from statics, then get transferred into video. "Ugly" (problem-agitating, lo-fi) ads win new audiences; polished ads read as ads.
- ## Principles
  - Test creatives, not audiences (Reddit consensus on her teaching; her own framework) — audiences are platform-managed; creative is the differentiator.
  - Learn on statics, scale on video: statics give early messaging learnings cheaply; winning messages then move into video.
  - "Ugly ad" = lean into the problem; the more you agitate the problem, the more effective (esp. for new audiences).
  - Iterate the winner: 10 key iterations AFTER finding a winning creative — iterate, don't restart.
  - Hooks for Meta (2025): 1) go against tradition (call out the tradition), 2) conversational hooks ("No because...", "Wait why is this...", "Ok this is insane...") — hyper-authentic, take a minute to process so viewers don't scroll past, 3) viral product hook ("I saw this on TikTok..."; frame product around a trending topic), 4) product reviews, 5) founder's story.
- ## Frameworks
  - Creative test formats: benefit image graphic, us-vs-them, headline ads, ugly statics, testimonial statics; video: ugly 1-shot 1-take video, founder's ad, stock footage with native platform fonts.
  - Budget-scaled testing structure (low budget $5k-30k/mo): 1 ASC campaign; test 1-3 new creatives per week; cap total creatives ~10; let winners run unless they burn budget.
- ## Decision rules
  - IF budget is $5k-30k/mo THEN use one ASC shopping campaign and test 1-3 new creatives/week (Denney — EMPIRICAL, T2).
  - IF early in testing THEN lead with statics to learn messaging cheaply, then port learnings into video (Denney — HEURISTIC, T2).
  - IF a creative wins THEN iterate it 10 ways before testing brand-new concepts (Denney — HEURISTIC, T2).
  - IF the audience is new/cold THEN test an "ugly" problem-agitating ad before polished brand creative (Denney — HEURISTIC, T2).
  - IF every ad sounds like an ad THEN switch to conversational hooks ("Wait why is this...") that take a second to process (Denney — HEURISTIC, T2).
- ## Metrics — creative-level CTR/CPA, hook retention at 3s, iterate on winning ads' fatigue curves; cap creative count when account budget is small.
- ## Failure modes — testing audiences instead of creatives; testing too many creatives for budget (diluted learnings); killing winners early on hunches; polishing instead of agitating; ignoring creative fatigue.
- ## Conditions — Meta-first ecommerce/performance marketing; budget ≥$5k/mo for meaningful creative testing; applies to DTC and lead-gen; less relevant for brand-only campaigns.
- ## Limitations — her $100M spend claim is self-reported (T3); platform-specific (Meta-focused, 2024-25 era rules shift); YouTube/TikTok testing structures differ.
