- ## Beliefs — The stay-or-scroll decision is made in 0.3-0.8 seconds, faster than conscious thought. The hook is not the most important part of the video — for most viewers it is the only part evaluated. Improving first-second retention from 50% to 70% effectively doubles the audience for the rest of the video.
- ## Empirical findings
  - 50-70% of viewers who will leave a video leave within the first 1-2 seconds (ClipSpeed analysis of platform retention graphs — EMPIRICAL, T2).
  - Optimal hook length: 1.5-3 seconds (Postigniter, 10,000+ viral Shorts analyzed).
  - Layered hooks (visual + auditory + textual) boost 3-second holds ~3x vs single-element intros (Hootsuite 2026 analysis via Postigniter — EMPIRICAL, T2).
  - Ads delivering the core message within the first 3 seconds achieved 60% more total retention (Billo TikTok ad performance review); nearly half of viewers who pass 3 seconds watch the full 30 seconds (Billo — EMPIRICAL, T2).
  - 60%+ of mobile views occur without sound → visual/textual redundancy required (Postigniter).
  - High-performing Shorts average one cut every 2-4 seconds; 15-30s is the sweet spot (Opus — EMPIRICAL, T2).
  - Platform windows differ: TikTok ~0.8-1s; YouTube Shorts 1.5-2s (more patience, curiosity + counter-intuitive work); Reels most visual — first frame aesthetics matter most (ClipSpeed).
- ## Hook formula menu (with conditions)
  - Mid-action opener (entertainment/storytelling) — start in medias res, brain fills the gap.
  - Contradiction (educational) — cognitive dissonance demands resolution; specificity adds credibility; verbal + text overlay for 90% comprehension.
  - Direct promise (how-to/value) — specific measurable outcome, e.g. "This 5-second edit doubled my watch time."
  - Numbered list (tech/tips) — numbers signal digestibility.
  - Text-overlay hook (sound-off) — 5-8 words, high-contrast, on screen 2+ seconds.
- ## Decision rules
  - IF the first frame contains setup, logo, or dead air THEN cut it — that's where the steepest drop lives (retention data — EMPIRICAL, T2).
  - IF the video is for TikTok THEN the hook must land in ~1 second; IF YouTube Shorts THEN ~1.5-2 seconds is available (ClipSpeed — EMPIRICAL, T2).
  - IF the viewer may watch muted THEN every claim must survive as on-screen text (Postigniter — EMPIRICAL, T2).
  - IF any 5-second stretch lacks a visual/verbal pattern break THEN rewrite the beat (Opus — EMPIRICAL, T2).
  - IF testing hooks THEN produce 2-3 hook variants of one body and read the 3-second retention rate as the score (Denney + TOVP practitioners — HEURISTIC, T2).
- ## Failure modes — clickbait promises the payoff doesn't deliver (algorithms penalize mismatch); text overload (>7 words); single-element hooks that cap at ~60% retention; writing for second 5 before surviving second 1.
- ## Conditions — applies to short-form feeds (TikTok, Reels, Shorts) and in-feed video ads; long-form YouTube and mid-roll differ.
- ## Limitations — vendor/tool data with incentive bias; retention baselines shift with platform algorithm changes; 2026-era figures may decay.
