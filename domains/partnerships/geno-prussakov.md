- ## Beliefs — Affiliate marketing fails most often at setup, not execution (1,000+ programs analyzed). Affiliates are independent marketers — you manage the program, never the affiliates. Money is not the only or best motivator; intrinsic motivation, tools, and relationships retain top affiliates.
- ## Principles — Don't treat affiliates as employees (no threats, no termination culture); don't assume all affiliates are fraudulent (paranoia kills recruitment); admit mistakes (accountability builds trust); communicate and incentivize, don't command.
- ## Frameworks — 7 mistakes of new affiliate managers; 8 setup-phase mistakes (terms, creatives, tracking, commission strategy, recruiter plan, fraud policy, communication plan, ongoing management).
- ## Processes — program setup: define commission structure → write terms → build creatives → set tracking → recruit first affiliates personally → manage as partners → iterate on data.
- ## Heuristics — if you'd manage them like staff, you've already lost them; if you can't name your top 10 affiliates and what they need, you're not managing the program.
- ## Metrics — EPC (earnings per click), per-affiliate revenue, recruitment rate, affiliate retention, fraud rate, program share of total revenue.
- ## Decision rules
  - IF program is new THEN recruit 10-20 high-quality affiliates personally before scaling outreach. (HEURISTIC, T2)
  - IF affiliates underperform THEN check commission vs market + communication before blaming affiliates. (HEURISTIC, T2)
  - IF fraud is suspected THEN fix tracking/policy; don't presume the whole channel is fraudulent. (FRAMEWORK, T1)
  - IF top affiliates churn THEN check intrinsic motivators (tools, recognition, relationship), not just payout. (FRAMEWORK, T1)
  - IF launching without terms/creatives/tracking THEN pause: setup mistakes are the #1 cause of program death. (FRAMEWORK, T1)
- ## Failure modes — employee-treatment of affiliates; smarter-than-affiliates arrogance; fraud paranoia; money-only incentives; failing to admit mistakes; losing focus (mistake #6).
- ## Conditions — programs with commissionable products and existing affiliate supply; his advice spans B2B SaaS content affiliates to DTC coupon/promo affiliates.
- ## Limitations — T2; essays from 2009/2018 — mechanics dated but principles stable; no published ROI stats.
