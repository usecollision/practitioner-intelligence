- ## Beliefs — Influencer marketing without a CFO-grade measurement layer is reach theater. Creator programs must be measured like any media channel: lift studies, exposure groups, spend-mix diagnosis, and comparison against paid benchmarks.
- ## Principles — Separate activity from business movement (time-series + lift + MMM inputs); evaluate creators by value created, not follower count; index organic creator impact against paid media; each program's learnings feed the next plan.
- ## Frameworks — SocialAI measurement stack: brand/campaign lift (awareness, consideration, message association, exposed-audience movement); creator ROI (per-creator value, format scale decisions); spend-mix diagnosis (creator vs paid vs content); paid-benchmark comparison (CPM, reach, creative performance, audience quality, frequency, CAC, ROAS).
- ## Processes — define campaign outcomes → set exposure groups → run creator content + paid amplification → measure lift + per-creator ROI vs paid benchmarks → re-allocate budget and creator mix → feed learnings into next plan.
- ## Heuristics — if a creator's content underperforms your organic baseline, don't scale it — re-brief or cut; if you can't name the benchmark comparison for a creator spend, you're not measuring.
- ## Metrics — lift (awareness/consideration/message association), creator CPM/CPC/CPE vs paid, ROAS/CAC by activation type, audience quality (exposed vs control), spend mix.
- ## Decision rules
  - IF no measurement layer exists THEN benchmark creator performance against paid CPM/ROAS before scaling any creator. (FRAMEWORK, T2)
  - IF creator reach is huge but engagement/outcomes lag benchmarks THEN treat reach claims as low confidence; test with small budgets first. (HEURISTIC, T2)
  - IF goal = brand movement THEN run lift studies with exposed vs unexposed groups; don't rely on vanity metrics. (FRAMEWORK, T2)
  - IF a creator format wins on ROI THEN scale that format across creators before adding new creators. (HEURISTIC, T2)
  - IF creator content underperforms organic baseline twice THEN re-brief or terminate before spending more. (HEURISTIC, T2)
- ## Failure modes — paying for reach, not outcomes; no control groups; scaling unmeasured creators; treating influencers as employees (brief rigidity killing authenticity).
- ## Conditions — brand/agency budgets that can support measurement; applies B2C and DTC heavily, B2B lighter.
- ## Limitations — T2/T3: vendor marketing material; specific ROI percentages and SocialAI efficacy claims unverified; no independent audits.
