## Beliefs
- Get the first thousand customers without paid methods — learn messaging/positioning first (HEURISTIC, T1).
- Paid media amplifies what already works organically; it's a booster, not the engine (T1).
## Principles
- Owned + paid media in tandem: test content organically, scale winners with paid (TACTIC, T1).
- Audience specificity: write/design for 3 concrete personas (founder-no-money, $50M CMO, early investor) (T1).
- Grandma test: product page copy must be instantly comprehensible (T1).
## Heuristics
- Milestones: ~$5k/day (up to $10k) in paid-attributed revenue before scaling hard (HEURISTIC, T1).
- Low CPM = platform doesn't like the creative; CTR = whether people like the message (diagnostic, T1).
- First $5k/day forces you to understand customer needs (T1).
## Decision rules
- IF <1,000 customers organic THEN don't scale paid (T1).
- IF CPM low + CTR low THEN fix creative/message, not audience (T1).
- IF organic content performs well THEN put paid behind the winners (T1).
- IF copy fails grandma test THEN rewrite before spending (T1).
## Failure modes
- Pumping paid into unproven messaging (T1).
- Misreading low CPM as audience problem (T1).
- "Invisible cash bleeds" in ops/supply chain eating margins (T2, Portless interview).
## Conditions
- Consumer brands, Shopify-based, founder-led; scales to $50M+ CMOs (T1).
