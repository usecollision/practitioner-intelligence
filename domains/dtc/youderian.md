## Beliefs
- "Being great at paid is less about top-notch ROAS and more about building a business model that supports ads as a major line item" (EMPIRICAL, T1).
- The P&L anatomy is the real edge: COGS + overhead, not the ad account (T1).
## Principles
- Winners at paid have mediocre ROAS (2.5x vs 4.0x survey-wide) but fat gross margins (63.7%) and lean overhead (16.6%) vs others (55.1% COGS / 21.7% overhead) (EMPIRICAL, T1).
- DTC-primary beats Amazon-primary: revenue +65% faster (30.2% vs 18.3%), GM 52.7% vs 41.9%; 91% of DTC operators love it vs 17% for Amazon (EMPIRICAL, T1).
## Heuristics
- Healthy DTC gross margin benchmark ~50%+; overhead <20% (HEURISTIC, T1 — 2026 survey medians).
## Decision rules
- IF gross margin < ~50% THEN fix COGS/pricing before scaling ads (T1).
- IF overhead > ~20% of revenue THEN cut fixed costs before more spend (T1).
- IF evaluating channel mix THEN weight DTC over marketplace when margin/control matter (T1).
- IF ROAS is great but margins thin THEN the business is fragile; paid profit is illusory (T1).
## Failure modes
- Optimizing ROAS while the P&L bleeds (T1).
- Amazon-first margin erosion (T1).
## Conditions
- US DTC, 7-8 figure stores; survey = 300 stores, $3.5B combined (T1).
