## Beliefs
- Dollar-in to dollar-out ("golden ratio") is the fundamental ad-economics discipline (FRAMEWORK, T2).
- Profitable Facebook funnels = video ads + anchored offer + backend (email/upsells) (T2).
## Principles
- "Anchor the sale, seed the offer" in ad creative (TACTIC, T2).
- The ultimate upsell + email backend converts one-time buyers into repeat purchasers (T2).
- Video ads do double duty: front-end acquisition + engagement (T2).
## Frameworks
- MER/golden ratio: total revenue ÷ total ad spend as the macro health number (FRAMEWORK, T2).
- 2x2 ad method + deep-funnel campaign structure (T2).
## Decision rules
- IF blended revenue:spend ratio below your target THEN cut spend until creative/offer fixed (T2).
- IF no backend (email/upsell) THEN expect acquisition-only economics; add backend before scaling (T2).
- IF ad creative underperforms THEN iterate on hook/offer anchoring, not audiences (T2).
## Failure modes
- Judging ads on ROAS alone while ignoring blended ratio (T2).
- No repeat-purchase engine → CAC never pays back (T2).
- Offer weakness hidden by creative spend (T2).
## Conditions
- Offer-led DTC with video-native creatives; heavy email backend; T2 confidence — much of the material is promotional course content.
