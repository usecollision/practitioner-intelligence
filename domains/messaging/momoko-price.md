## Beliefs
- You cannot optimize "a page" — you optimize a funnel: landing on a page → clicking an element → next page → conversion (OPINION, T2 — Copywriter Club podcast #17).
- Copy decisions should be quant-backed: analytics baseline before any copy or design spend (OPINION, T2 — client testimonial: "Every marketer should get these insights before spending a dime on copy or design").
- If the client doesn't know baseline conversion rate and traffic, tests are not remotely conclusive (OPINION, T2).

## Principles
- Redirect page-focused requests to funnel-focused engagements (PRINCIPLE, T2).
- Funnel mapping = quant + qual combined: analytics tells where people drop; research tells why (PRINCIPLE, T2; master-map framing).
- Copy is a treatment built from research and deployed as a testable hypothesis (PRINCIPLE, T2).

## Frameworks
- Conversion Funnel & Copy Report: pick a funnel (entry → checkout/transaction), map pages, assess both analytics and content against a report structure, find biggest problem spots, generate simple tests targeting them (FRAMEWORK, T2).
- Funnel mapping method (Kantan): quant (analytics) + qual (copy/message research) per funnel stage (FRAMEWORK, T2; details of the public method pages 404'd — method reconstructed from podcast).

## Processes
- Onboarding: ask client for baseline analytics (CTR, conversion goal/rate, traffic volume, testing tool, integration state, target + what's failed so far) — clients who can't answer get redirected or ghost (PROCESS, T2).
- Engagement flow: analytics audit ($1k checklist: referral spam, test-tool integration, GA setup) → funnel audit → generate treatments + testing guidelines → optional pre-test QA round (paid) (PROCESS, T2).

## Heuristics
- Traffic + baseline conversion rate are the two gatekeeping questions (HEURISTIC, T2).
- If a client says "optimize this page" they don't understand their funnel — redirect or walk (HEURISTIC, T2).
- Her first pass on a funnel two CRO agencies failed on got a testing win + higher-ticket mix (HEURISTIC/TESTIMONIAL, T3 — vendor testimonial).

## Tactics
- Send a standard question pack straight to the analytics at inquiry time (TACTIC, T2).
- Sell funnel audit as the first engagement for any client (TACTIC, T2).
- Offer pre-test QA as a paid checkpoint to avoid being "on the hook" for weeks-long test cycles (TACTIC, T2).

## Tools
- Google Analytics, testing tools (client's), Kantan funnel-map report structure (TOOL, T2).

## Inputs
- Analytics access, funnel definition, page list, baseline rates, traffic volume, testing capability (INPUT, T2).

## Outputs
- Conversion funnel & copy report; prioritized test list; copy treatments; QA checkpoints (OUTPUT, T2).

## Metrics
- Baseline conversion rate, funnel drop-off per stage, test lift, higher-ticket share (METRIC, T2).

## Decision rules
- If client lacks baseline analytics → no engagement; they must sort measurement first (DECISION RULE, T2).
- If traffic is too low for conclusive tests → don't promise lift; set expectations or skip experimentation (DECISION RULE, T2).
- Focus tests on the funnel's biggest problem spots identified by the report (DECISION RULE, T2).

## Failure modes
- Optimizing pages in isolation instead of funnels (WARNING, T2).
- Writing copy with no baseline → unmeasurable work (WARNING, T2).
- Being on the hook for weeks-long test cycles without scope control (WARNING, T2 — her own past struggle).

## Contrarian beliefs
- Copywriters should act like analysts: gate projects on measurement readiness (OPINION, T2).

## Examples
- PPC sales funnel where two specialized CRO agencies failed; her first attempt won in testing and increased higher-ticket share (EXAMPLE, T3 — from her site's testimonials; vendor-sourced).

## Conditions
- DTC/e-com and SaaS funnels with measurable traffic (CONDITION, T2).
- Requires analytics maturity; fails when measurement is absent (CONDITION, T2).

## Limitations
- Method specifics (funnel-mapping templates) are behind her course; public detail limited (LIMITATION, T3).
- Testimonial evidence is vendor-curated (LIMITATION, T2).
