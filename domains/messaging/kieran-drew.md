## Beliefs
- Not all content is created equal: one post had 1000x the impact of a year of "crap" posts — because people follow people (OPINION, T2).
- Emotional connection beats gimmicks, tricks, and "zombified AI-generated posts" (OPINION, T2).
- Writing is the greatest opportunity to build a brand/business; every word becomes a robot working for you (OPINION, T2).

## Principles
- Great content gives readers three things: actionable advice (authority), personality (differentiation), storytelling (connection) (PRINCIPLE, T2).
- Simple frameworks + systems beat talent; consistency compounds (PRINCIPLE, T2).

## Frameworks
- S.U.C.K.S. editing checklist: Specific? Unique, useful, undeniable? Clear, curious, conversational? Kept simple? Structured? (FRAMEWORK, T2 — LinkedIn, 2024-02).
- The Tinder Test: summarize your unique message in one sentence (FRAMEWORK, T2).
- One True Fan method: deep understanding of one reader so every word has magnetic pull (FRAMEWORK, T2).
- High Impact Writing system: content-idea checklists, hook checklists, short/long-form checklists (FRAMEWORK, T2).

## Processes
- Write → edit against S.U.C.K.S. checklist → package idea per platform format → reuse via systems (PROCESS, T2).
- Grows on 3-4 platforms with 2-3 hrs/week via reusable frameworks (PROCESS, T2).

## Heuristics
- When editing, run every line through the S.U.C.K.S. letters (HEURISTIC, T2).
- If a reader can't summarize your message in one sentence, it's not clear enough (HEURISTIC, T2).

## Tactics
- Checklists for ideas, hooks, short/long form (TACTIC, T2).
- Personal story + proof pattern in his own content (TACTIC, T2).

## Tools
- X/LinkedIn, course modules, checklists (TOOL, T2).

## Inputs
- Audience understanding (One True Fan), personal experience, niche knowledge (INPUT, T2).

## Outputs
- Short/long-form posts, frameworks, checklists, course (OUTPUT, T2).

## Metrics
- Engagement (post impact), platform growth, time-to-produce (2-3 hrs/week claim) (METRIC, T2).

## Decision rules
- If a sentence isn't specific/clear/simple → rewrite before publishing (DECISION RULE, T2).
- If you're posting generic templates → stop; lead with personality + story (DECISION RULE, T2, inferred).

- AI-generated generic content: "zombified" noise (WARNING, T2 — opinion, not data).
