## Beliefs
- Your email list is your biggest business asset — email builds a direct, authentic relationship on your terms (OPINION, T2).
- Stories and personality beat "perfect" copy every time; people don't want polished, they want relatable (OPINION, T2).
- Email copywriting is generally "creative writing with a call to action" — different from other copy (OPINION, T2).

## Principles
- Being unapologetically yourself in email is the differentiator (PRINCIPLE, T2).
- The more human, specific, and real, the more resonance (PRINCIPLE, T2).
- Voice converts: personality drives opens, clicks, and buys (PRINCIPLE, T2).

## Frameworks
- Inbox Hero email makeover framework: subject line → preview text → greeting → start of email → tone → economy/clarity → punch/personality/storytelling → readability (FRAMEWORK, T2).
- Story Hero (storytelling deep-dive) (FRAMEWORK, T2).

## Processes
- Write conversational email with personality → edit against the Inbox Hero elements → maintain the voice across cadence without burning out the list or yourself (PROCESS, T2).

## Heuristics
- Subject line + preview text are the open gate; personality is the retention engine (HEURISTIC, T2).
- Use AI as an assistant for conversational alternatives — not as the author (HEURISTIC, T2).

## Tactics
- Makeover-style editing of real emails against the element checklist (TACTIC, T2).
- Humor and personal specifics in every email (TACTIC, T2).

## Tools
- Talking Shrimp courses, email platforms, ChatGPT for conversational rewrites (TOOL, T2).

## Inputs
- List relationship, personal stories, brand voice definition (INPUT, T2).

## Outputs
- Emails that get opened, clicked, bought from; voice guides (OUTPUT, T2).

## Metrics
- Open rate, click rate, reply/engagement, sales (METRIC, T2).

## Decision rules
- If an email sounds like "perfect" marketing copy → rewrite it more human, more specific (DECISION RULE, T2).
- If you can't write conversationally in English → use ChatGPT to generate 20 slangy alternatives and pick (DECISION RULE, T2 — her own FAQ advice).

## Failure modes
- Polished, generic, corporate-sounding emails (WARNING, T2).
- Burning the list (or yourself) by over-emailing without personality (WARNING, T2).
- AI-written emails that lose your voice (WARNING, T2).

## Contrarian beliefs
- Personality is a conversion strategy, not a style preference (OPINION, T2).

## Examples
- Her own list as the canonical example of voice-driven email (EXAMPLE, T2, self-reported).
- Inbox Hero students' makeovers (EXAMPLE, T3, vendor claim).

## Conditions
- Email marketing for B2B and B2C; strongest for audiences who tolerate/expect personality (creator, agency, service businesses) (CONDITION, T2).
- Less applicable to ultra-formal enterprise/regulated comms (CONDITION, T2, inferred).

## Limitations
- Voice-first approach lacks explicit research/testing discipline of Wiebe/Price (LIMITATION, T2).
- Self-reported results (LIMITATION, T2).
