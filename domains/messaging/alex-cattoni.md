## Beliefs
- Best copy combines proven direct-response principles with modern branding and storytelling (OPINION, T2).
- The industry has a "dangerous divide" between old-school sales copy and modern branding — she bridges it (OPINION, T2).
- Hooks are the most important skill for engagement and sales in today's content landscape (OPINION, T2).

## Principles
- Structure beats inspiration: frameworks and templates make copy teachable and repeatable (PRINCIPLE, T2).
- Brand voice must feel authentic, not fake or forced (PRINCIPLE, T2).
- Emojis capture attention with fewer words, but don't overdo it (PRINCIPLE, T2).

## Frameworks
- 99 Hook Templates: fill-in-the-blank hooks for social captions, short-form video, headlines, subject lines, ads, YouTube titles, sales/landing pages (FRAMEWORK, T2).
- Posse Eye Framework: 4 core storytelling/copywriting elements for a distinct brand voice (Rally Cry, Brand Promise, etc.) (FRAMEWORK, T2, partial details).

## Processes
- Brand Voice Guide process: step-by-step, customizable, using real brands; output in a few hours (PROCESS, T2).

## Heuristics
- Hooks go at the start of every asset: first sentence of captions, top of video, headline, subject line (HEURISTIC, T2).

## Tactics
- Fill-in-the-blank hook templates for velocity (TACTIC, T2).
- Teaching copy through community (Copy Posse crew) and courses (TACTIC, T2).

## Tools
- Copy Posse Academy, hook template PDFs, YouTube/Instagram education content (TOOL, T2).

## Inputs
- Brand personality inputs, offer, audience context (INPUT, T2, inferred).

## Outputs
- Hook libraries, brand voice guides, educational content (OUTPUT, T2).

## Metrics
- Engagement (views, saves, shares), conversions from her students' campaigns (METRIC, T3, unverified).

## Decision rules
- When starting any asset → write the hook first using a template that matches the intent (attention vs curiosity vs specificity) (DECISION RULE, T2, inferred).
- When defining voice → derive from the 4 Posse Eye elements before writing (DECISION RULE, T2).

## Failure modes
- Overdoing emojis / gimmicks; sounding fake or forced (WARNING, T2).
- Learning copy by memorizing sales pages instead of systems (WARNING, T2, inferred).

## Contrarian beliefs
- Modern marketing needs "caring" — de-douchify the internet; persuasion without being salesy (OPINION, T2).

## Examples
- 300k+ students/customers in Copy Posse community (EXAMPLE, T3, vendor claim).
- Partnered launches for "transformational authors" — specifics UNVERIFIED (EXAMPLE, T3).

## Conditions
- Best for creator/online-business education contexts and short-form social copy (CONDITION, T2).
- Less applicable to enterprise B2B long-form technical messaging (CONDITION, T2, inferred).

## Limitations
- Educator content: mostly frameworks for learners, limited documented enterprise case evidence (LIMITATION, T2).
- Template approach risks generic output without research discipline (her method lacks the Wiebe/Price research-first emphasis) (LIMITATION, T2, inferred).
