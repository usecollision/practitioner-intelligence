## Beliefs
- "Design the product around the price," not the reverse (FRAMEWORK, T1).
- 72% of new products fail to meet financial goals — mostly avoidable monetization failures (EMPIRICAL, T1).
- Last-minute pricing is the root cause: monetization must be part of product development (T1).
## Frameworks
- Four failure patterns (FRAMEWORK, T1):
  1. Feature shock — too many features, overpriced, no segment resonance.
  2. Minivation — right product, priced too low, revenue left on table.
  3. Hidden gem — customers love it but no monetization path (no value metric).
  4. Undead — "me-too" nobody asked for.
## Principles
- Value metric must track customer-perceived value (usage/outcomes), enabling expansion (T1).
- Price anchored to WTP data + feature preference, before launch (T1).
## Decision rules
- IF feature count bloats across segments THEN cut to segment-relevant sets (feature shock guard) (T1).
- IF product loved but revenue flat THEN build premium tier/add-on on the loved capability (hidden gem guard) (T1).
- IF pricing decided post-build THEN re-run WTP research and repackage before launch (T1).
- IF no value metric exists THEN define "what the customer gets more of as they succeed" (T2).
## Failure modes
- Pricing treated as "dirty work" deferred to launch (T1).
- Feature shock from cramming features (T1).
- Minivation from fear of overpricing (T1).
## Conditions
- New product launches + existing product monetization; B2B and B2C (T1).
