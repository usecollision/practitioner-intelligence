## Beliefs
- 2025 = the year everyone lost confidence in their pricing: 1,800+ pricing changes among top-500 SaaS/AI = 3.6 per company (EMPIRICAL, T1).
- "Pricing problems are value clarity problems" — if customers don't understand what they pay for, no model feels right (OPINION, T1).
## Principles
- Don't abandon broken pricing; fix it (T1).
- Track trends with data: credits +126% YoY in 2025, then re-bundling toward simplicity in 2026 (EMPIRICAL, T1).
- Hybrid pricing (seat base + usage expansion) emerging as the answer to both flat-expansion and forecast-pain problems (EMPIRICAL, T1).
## Frameworks
- Credits 2x2: customer-friendly vs vendor-friendly × cost-based vs value-based (FRAMEWORK, T1).
- B2B pricing challenge map (230+ companies): flat fee → no expansion revenue; seats → not future-proof w/ AI; usage → hard to forecast; hybrid → hard to explain (FRAMEWORK, T1).
## Decision rules
- IF flat-fee plan THEN add premium edition/add-ons or fair-use policy (T1).
- IF seat-based THEN move to hybrid with usage-based expansion (T1).
- IF usage/credit-based THEN give admins spend visibility + control (T1).
- IF hybrid is confusing THEN simplify credit model (fewer actions), document pricing for LLMs/buyers (T1).
- IF AI add-on is a separate $4-10/user charge THEN expect the market to bundle it into core pricing +$2.50-5/user (EMPIRICAL, T1).
## Failure modes
- Changing price too often (3.6x/yr) signals instability; customers lose confidence (T1).
- Usage pricing without forecast ability → CFO revolt (T1).
## Conditions
- SaaS/AI with transparent pricing, top-500 scale; trends may lag in mid-market (T2).
