# PLATFORM PANEL — Pinterest & Threads (2025-26)

## Pinterest — the platform
- **Scale/shift:** 600M MAU (Q2 2025, +11% YoY). CEO Bill Ready: "an AI-powered shopping assistant for 600 million consumers." Revenue mix flipped: Q3 2022 equal awareness/consideration/conversion → Q3 2025 two-thirds from driving sales. (FACT — Pinterest earnings via Shopify guide, T2)
- **Intent:** 90% of weekly pinners use Pinterest to make purchase decisions; pinners 3x more likely to make an online purchase each week (platform/Sprout data, T2). Users save 1.5B+ pins/week across 10M+ boards (Klaviyo, T3).
- **Search duality:** Pinterest is its own search engine AND feeds Google/Bing/DuckDuckGo; pins/boards are now surfacing in LLM/AI snippets (Sprout 2026 — HYPOTHESIS/T2, young).
- **SEO mechanics (multi-source convergence — T2):** claim website (unlocks analytics + priority in search); profile name "Brand | Main Keyword" <50 chars; board titles keyword-first and specific ("Modern Farmhouse Living Rooms" > "Home Decor"); pin descriptions 100-500 chars, first sentence = primary keyword + hook, front-loaded; 3-5 hashtags max; alt text ~125 chars with keyword; 2:3 aspect (1000×1500) dominates; text overlay ≤40% of image; fresh pins > re-pins; consistent cadence beats bursts.
- **Analytics funnel (agency intel — T3):** impressions (reach) → closeups (relevance/creative) → saves (intent, most important) → outbound clicks (traffic) → estimated conversions (Pinterest Tag). Engagement rate = (saves+clicks)/impressions. Weekly 7/14/28-day snapshots; creative diagnostic top-20% vs bottom-20% pins.

## Pinterest — decision rules from 2026 analytics practice (T3, aggregator-consensus)
1. CTR > 2x account median AND save rate > median → promote via paid or pin to priority boards.
2. High impressions, low closeups → change creative hook or first 30 chars of description.
3. Saves high, outbound clicks low → fix description/CTA; verify landing page matches the pin's promise.
4. Both closeups and saves below median after 2 iterations → pause that creative pattern.
5. Don't scale paid until a pin clears ≥2 organic iterations with measurable lift.
6. Benchmarks are account-relative: CTR ≥1.5x your historical median before significant paid budget.

## Pinterest — failure knowledge (T2/T3)
- Treating Pinterest like Instagram (square lifestyle shots, no keyword targeting) — it's search, not feed.
- Old playbook is dead: 15-20 pins/day repinning, 80/20 own/others ratio, group boards, Tailwind Tribes.
- Quantity ≠ repinning: portfolio logic still applies — top 1% of pins = 50% of impressions/clicks over 90 days (Tailwind study) → pin at volume with FRESH original pins, expect most to do nothing.
- Keyword stuffing, low-res images, inconsistent pinning, re-sharing the same pin repeatedly, unclaimed website — all suppress ranking.
- Shopping failures: catalog sync breaks (feed inactive, products stuck in review/approval), 24-48h tagging delays, checkout events not matching catalog product IDs — check feed status before blaming creative.
- Measuring impressions instead of saves/outbound clicks/sales.

## Threads — the platform
- **Scale:** 175-200M+ MAU (mid-2025) → ~400-500M by 2026 (NYT: 500M, "as popular as X") (T2, ranges reflect measurement dates). 500-char posts; no hashtags — topic tags; algorithmic feed; search improving; ads rolling out 2025 with intentionally low volume.
- **Algorithm (agency consensus — T3):** rewards engagement velocity (replies/reposts in first ~30 min), conversation depth, account engagement rate, cross-platform signals (Instagram presence boosts Threads distribution), topical relevance. Suppresses: repetitive promo, external links (deprioritized), engagement bait, negative feedback (hide/mute/report).
- **Content:** 70/20/10 mix (70% conversational: opinions, questions, commentary; 20% value: tips, data, mini-tutorials; 10% promotional). Over-produced content underperforms — "if it sounds like it came from a marketing deck, rewrite it." Reply to every comment, especially early (ClickUp pattern). Topic tags + searchable phrasing = Threads "SEO."
- **Metrics:** replies per post (primary), reach, reposts, follower growth 5-10%/mo in early stages, profile visits from Instagram, brand mention volume. Engagement ~5-6% claims; 3-5x X/LinkedIn engagement claims for early adopters (T3 vendor).
- **Positioning vs X:** Threads = relationship/community, calmer, brand-safety-positive; X = news/megaphone, real-time trends. 92% of marketers say influencer content outperforms brand accounts (T3).

## Threads — failure knowledge (T3)
- Auto-cross-posting identical Instagram captions → reads as spam, suppressed.
- Broadcasting without replying — the engagement mechanism IS the distribution.
- Leading with external links → deprioritized.
- Polished brand-broadcast voice — the opposite of what the feed rewards.

## Conditions (when each platform is correct)
- **Pinterest:** B2C/visual/purchase-intent (home, fashion, food, crafts, design, DTC); content businesses with image assets; seasonal/planning categories; strong for women-skewing and Gen Z shopping demographics. Weak: text-heavy enterprise B2B, services without visuals.
- **Threads:** brand voice/community plays, founder-led personal brands, audiences already on Instagram, thought-leadership content; launch commentary + customer conversation. Weak: pure conversion channels, no-audience cold starts (discovery is follower/algorithm-bound).
