# PANEL — Launch Operations (product-launch-playbook)

## Consensus (T1: Dietrich PESO, Meerman Scott newsjacking, Zitron pitching, Rachitsky GTM stages)
- **PESO order with designed handoffs:** owned content is the foundation, shared distributes, earned validates, paid amplifies what already works. Design the handoffs before the calendar (Dietrich).
- **Sequencing:** exclusive press/community gets it first; broad channels follow within hours; owned channels on time, never early.
- **Stage gates:** pre-PMF, first customers come from founder-led non-scalable channels (network, strategic outbound, investor intros, communities) — "none of these scale, that's why they work" (Rachitsky). Post-PMF, loops and paid amplification.
- **Launch is won in weeks 1-2, not day 1:** momentum push (customer stories, data recaps, follow-up webinars/AMAs) + a retrospective that updates the playbook.

## Disagreement & resolution
1. **Big-bang vs rolling thunder:** big-bang for big news with an existing audience; rolling/staged for niche or complex products (T2).
2. **Press release dead vs alive:** releases work as owned records + newsjacked second-paragraph content, not as coverage generators (Zitron/Meerman Scott — T1).
3. **Newsjacking vs planned campaigns:** keep the PESO system, leave reaction slots (newsjacking lanes) inside the calendar (T1).

## Failure knowledge (T1/T2)
- Launch day is the plan — no T-30 → T-7 build-up, the day lands flat.
- Assets discovered missing on launch day (no asset owner/checklist).
- Owned channels posting before embargo lifts.
- Spray-and-pray pitching: long/formatted/exclamation-heavy pitches deleted (Zitron — T1).
- No single north-star metric → "success by committee."
- Stopping at day 1; late newsjacking (24h+) → story has moved (Meerman Scott).
- PH launches without pre-assembled audience + 24h comment engagement staffed → buried (KWD).

## Collision Method (launch) — decision rules
1. IF owned/shared channels don't exist THEN build them before any earned-media ask (Dietrich, FRAMEWORK, T1).
2. IF pre-PMF THEN founder-led non-scalable channels only; no paid big-bang (Rachitsky, HEURISTIC, T1).
3. IF pitching a journalist THEN ≤150 words, plain text, one reason that reporter cares; tier-3 long-tail can take volume (Zitron, HEURISTIC, T1).
4. IF a breaking story has a credible tie and <4h internal approval THEN newsjack with second-paragraph content; else skip (Meerman Scott, HEURISTIC, T1).
5. IF launch tier is undecided THEN big-bang for big news + existing audience; rolling for niche/complex (synthesis, HEURISTIC, T2).
6. IF day-30 metrics miss thresholds THEN fix activation/positioning before the next launch — checklist efficiency is not the lesson (synthesis, HEURISTIC, T2).

## Metrics
- Primary: one north-star for the launch window (signups/demos/revenue/waitlist) with day-1/week-1/day-30 thresholds.
- Secondary: coverage quality + journalist reply rate (≥5% after 20 individualized pitches, else rewrite template), community activity, referral traffic, AI-citation mentions (2026: earned media feeds AI visibility — Dietrich, T2).
- Guardrail: no AVE/vanity metrics.
