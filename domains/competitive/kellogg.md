## Beliefs
- "The goal of competitive is not to produce research... The goal is to win deals" (T1).
- CI is an applied function: knowledge → sales plays → wins (T1).
## Principles
- CI includes product, but also competitor sales plays and company strategy (exec bios, likely plays) (T1).
- Comparison charts without documented scoring are subjective wallpaper (T1).
- Metric for CI: head-to-head win rate vs chosen competitors (HEURISTIC, T1).
## Frameworks
- Win-deals vs product-comparison vs holistic-research (three CI approaches; only win-deals pays) (FRAMEWORK, T1).
- The "Heisman" example: turn competitor's end-run play into your sales play using your strengths (EXAMPLE, T1).
## Decision rules
- IF a comparison chart lacks scoring documentation THEN fix or remove (T1).
- IF CI output has no sales play attached THEN it's research-for-knowledge's-sake; convert or cut (T1).
- IF competitor runs a known sales play THEN build a counter-play + reframe criteria (T1).
- IF you must choose CI focus THEN prioritize head-to-head competitors you actually lose to (T2).
## Failure modes
- Harvey Balls as the job description (T1).
- Reports nobody reads; no enablement loop (T1).
- Ignoring competitor sales plays and company strategy (T1).
## Conditions
- B2B with competitive sales cycles; needs field intel + win/loss feedback (T1).
