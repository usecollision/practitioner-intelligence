## Beliefs
- For product-led businesses, positioning is activation-led: the product experience IS the positioning (OPINION, T2; master-map framing).
- Growth comes from a system, not one-off tactics; 80% of results from 20% of components (Pareto) (OPINION, T2).
- High-touch → zero-touch: users must sign up, reach value, and upgrade without talking to you (PRINCIPLE, T2).

## Principles
- Order matters: optimizing onboarding without a clear offer is futile; scaling team without customer traction drains resources (PRINCIPLE, T2).
- The system is a living framework: full cycle at least once a year, up to 4x/year for fast growers (PRINCIPLE, T2).

## Frameworks
- ProductLed System: 3 phases × 3 components = 9: Phase 1 foundation (Strategy/Bullseye, User, Model/DEEP); Phase 2 self-serve unlock (Offer, Onboarding, Pricing); Phase 3 scale (EXPAND/retention etc.) (FRAMEWORK, T2).
- Bullseye Strategy Framework (clarity on what the company is really good at + what winning looks like) (FRAMEWORK, T2).
- DEEP Model Framework (decide freemium vs free trial, gamify the user journey) (FRAMEWORK, T2).

## Processes
- Strategy → User → Model → Offer → Onboarding → Pricing → scale components, in sequence (PROCESS, T2).

## Heuristics
- 80/20: master the 20% of components driving 80% of results (HEURISTIC, T2).
- "Become the obvious choice in your market" = goal state (HEURISTIC, T2).

## Tactics
- Zero-touch onboarding design; self-serve upgrade paths; product-qualified lead motions (TACTIC, T2, inferred).

## Tools
- ProductLed academy, playbooks, case-study templates (TOOL, T2).

## Inputs
- Company strengths, target user clarity, current model (freemium/trial), user journey data (INPUT, T2).

## Outputs
- ProductLed roadmap; activation/onboarding designs; pricing model choices (OUTPUT, T2).

## Metrics
- Activation, time-to-value, self-serve conversion, ARR (METRIC, T2, inferred).

## Decision rules
- If offer is unclear → fix offer before onboarding; if no traction → don't scale team (DECISION RULE, T2).
- If your product is adopted bottom-up → position via in-product experience and self-serve path, not sales narrative (DECISION RULE, T2, inferred).

## Failure modes
- High-touch sales motion on a PLG product (contradiction) (WARNING, T2, inferred).
- Optimizing pieces out of order (onboarding before offer) (WARNING, T2).

## Contrarian beliefs
- Positioning is less about the pitch and more about the product journey for PLG companies (OPINION, T2; his differentiation from narrative school).

- Product-led SaaS where users can self-serve; freemium/trial models (CONDITION, T2).
- Less relevant to high-ACV enterprise sales-led motions (CONDITION, T2, inferred).

## Limitations

- Frame is growth-system, not positioning-theory; light on competitive frame choice (LIMITATION, T2).
