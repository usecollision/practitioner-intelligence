## Beliefs
- Copy must be preceded by research: "Every marketer should get these insights before spending a dime on copy or design" (OPINION, T1 — kantan.io testimonials).
- "You can't optimize a page; you optimize a funnel" — reframe every request from page-level to funnel-level, because that's where money is made/lost (OPINION, T1 — Copywriter Club podcast).
- Clients who can't state their baseline conversion rate and traffic don't understand what they're asking for; those prerequisites filter out unserious engagements (OPINION, T1 — Copywriter Club).

## Principles
- Quant + qual combined: funnel analytics (where people drop off) must be paired with customer research (why) before writing (PRINCIPLE, T2 — Kantan method canon).
- Research → diagnosis → handful of simple tests; don't rewrite blindly (PRINCIPLE, T1 — Copywriter Club).

## Frameworks
- Kantan funnel mapping: map the funnel (entry → next step → ... → transaction), attach quantitative evidence (analytics, drop-off) to each stage, overlay qualitative voice-of-customer data (surveys, interviews, feedback), then write copy that targets the highest-leverage friction (FRAMEWORK, T2 — CXL "Funnel Mapping for Conversion Optimization" course canon; detail level T2).
- Conversion Funnel & Copy Report: structured report of the funnel's problem spots + recommended tests (OUTPUT, T1 — kantan.io).

## Processes
- Onboarding gate: before accepting a project, send a handful of questions forcing the client to verify baseline analytics: CTR, conversion rate, testing tool, analytics tool, integration status, baseline goal (PROCESS, T1 — Copywriter Club).
- Conversion funnel audit: (1) client picks the funnel; (2) get Google Analytics access; (3) navigate the funnel pages; (4) assess against a report structure; (5) find biggest problem spots; (6) generate a handful of simple tests targeting those spots (PROCESS, T1 — Copywriter Club).
- Deliver tests + deployment guidelines; client can run them solo or pay for QA oversight (PROCESS, T1).

## Heuristics
- The two big questions before any CRO work: traffic volume (enough for conclusive tests?) and baseline conversion rate (HEURISTIC, T1 — Copywriter Club).
- If the client ghosts on the analytics questions, the engagement wasn't real (HEURISTIC, T1).

## Tactics
- Redirect "optimize this page" requests into funnel conversations: "where are you losing people?" (TACTIC, T1).
- Phase out pure "write this page" projects; always lead with the audit (TACTIC, T1).

## Tools
- Google Analytics; A/B testing tools; funnel mapping worksheets (TOOL, T1).

## Inputs
- GA access; funnel definition; baseline metrics; (ideally) customer research data (INPUT, T1).

## Outputs
- Funnel audit report; prioritized test hypotheses; copy treatments; deployment guidelines (OUTPUT, T1).

## Metrics
- Conversion rate per funnel step; test lift; higher-ticket sales proportion (METRIC, T1 — kantan.io case: first test won, lifted higher-ticket share).

## Decision rules
- No baseline conversion rate + no traffic → refuse/redirect the engagement (DECISION RULE, T1 — Copywriter Club).
- Fix the funnel's biggest quantified drop-off with the simplest test first (DECISION RULE, T2).

## Failure modes
- Clients treating CRO as "rewrite this page and give me money" with no measurement (FAILURE, T1).
- Agencies failing on a funnel she then won on the first test — evidence that research-first beats experience-only (FAILURE/EXAMPLE, T1 — kantan.io testimonial: two specialized CRO agencies got no lift; her first test won).

## Contrarian beliefs
- Analytics literacy is a precondition for hiring a copywriter; most "optimize my page" requests are invalid (OPINION, T1).

## Examples
- PPC sales funnel where two prior CRO agencies produced no lift; her first test beat control and increased higher-ticket share (EXAMPLE, T1 — kantan.io).
- Clients: Intuit, Scotiabank, AT&T (EXAMPLE, T2 — Copywriter Club).

## Conditions
- Best for funnels with enough traffic to test (established products); not for zero-traffic pre-launch (CONDITION, T1).

## Limitations
- Requires analytics hygiene that many SMB clients lack (LIMITATION, T1).
- Her current practice (2026) shifted to non-profit impact analytics per kantan.io — the funnel-mapping method lives on via CXL course and her archives (LIMITATION, T1 — kantan.io homepage).
