# Ad Library Research — Panel

## Beliefs
- Competitive ad analysis "is most useful when you treat it like customer research, not creative shopping" (PrimeSpy). You are understanding what your market responds to and which objections competitors address — not collecting "good ads".
- Meta Ad Library is free, account-less, and shows every ACTIVE ad — the professional playbook starts there; paid spy tools add filters, archives, and workflow, not magic.
- "A spy tool can help you spot ads that look promising, but it cannot prove exact profitability" (PrimeSpy). Long-running ads + repeated variations + strong engagement are proxies only.

## Processes (AdsUploader playbook, condensed)
1. **Find every competitor ad**: direct search → regional pages (Brand USA vs Brand EU) → product-specific pages → parent company/sister brands → reverse from creative (distinctive copy line) → map the category with generic product searches to surface unknown entrants.
2. **Read their strategy from creative themes**: what pain points lead? lifestyle vs product shots? video vs static ratio? heavy social proof? "If every ad screams price, they're competing on cost. If craftsmanship, they're defending margin."
3. **Analyze like customer research**: what problem is the viewer already aware of? what promise is being made? what objections are being addressed? which messages appear often enough to be worth testing?
4. **Build the swipe file**: save ads with notes; Foreplay-class tools turn inspiration into organized creative work and briefs.
5. **Close the loop**: research only pays when it becomes ads in the auction — spot the winner, launch variants, repeat (AdsUploader).

## Heuristics
- Free-first ladder: Meta Ad Library for basics → paid (PrimeSpy $9.99/mo, AdSpy $149/mo, PowerAdSpy $69–399/mo) when manual research becomes the bottleneck; agencies may want swipe-file tools (Foreplay) or ecommerce validation (Minea).
- Page-likes threshold ~1,000+ to filter for serious advertisers when scraping at scale (Saraev).
- Saved creative is for analysis, never republishing (AdsUploader).
- Cross-platform: Google Ads Transparency Center, TikTok Creative Center, LinkedIn Ad Library cover non-Meta.

## Decision rules
- IF a competitor repeats similar imagery/copy across ads THEN infer it performs and treat as a validated angle worth testing (Shopify).
- IF an ad has run long or shows repeated variations THEN treat as a performance proxy (not proof) and test the angle yourself.
- IF every competitor ad leads with price THEN the market is competing on cost — differentiation must come from elsewhere (read strategy).
- IF you can't name the problem the ad's viewer is already aware of THEN you haven't analyzed the ad; go back to the user's point of view.
- IF research produces no testable hypothesis for the next campaign THEN stop researching; launch variants (AdsUploader).
- IF analyzing mobile THEN treat as triage; deep comparison needs desktop.

## Failure modes
- Creative shopping: becoming "a weaker version of your competitors" with a different logo (PrimeSpy).
- Copying protected creative (legal risk).
- Believing engagement/impressions proxies = profitability.
- Buying spy tools before defining the workflow ("If it only gives you more ads to scroll through, it adds noise" — PrimeSpy).
