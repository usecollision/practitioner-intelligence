# Account Intelligence & Intent — Panel

## Beliefs
- "Most ABM programs don't fail on strategy. They fail on the shelf life of the data underneath the strategy" (HG Insights). A target list built in January is misleading by June.
- Intent is "evidence to investigate; it does not prove that someone will buy" (Demandbase framing).
- "Intent without fit produces noise. Intent with fit produces a ranked list worth working" (HG Insights).

## Frameworks
- **Five data types** (HG Insights): firmographics (baseline fit, drifts slowly), technographics (what the account runs, 20k+ products tracked), IT spend (budget capacity), intent (active research), contract intelligence (renewal timing). Each answers a different "worth pursuing right now?" question.
- **F.I.R.E.** (ITSMA): Fit → Intent → Recency → Engagement for account prioritization; all four needed.
- **ABI stack** (HockeyStack): account as unit of analysis; unified first-party engagement + third-party intent/technographics/firmographics + buying committee mapping + news/event triggers; fit+engagement scoring.
- **Intent types** (G2/Bombora four-part taxonomy): zero-party (forms/surveys), first-party (your site/product/email), second-party (partner/marketplace), third-party (publisher networks, co-ops).

## Heuristics
- Intent decays fast: 0–7 days hot (immediate activation), 8–30 days moderate (still worth touch, urgency drops), beyond 30 days cold (Prospeo).
- Job-change signals are among the strongest triggers: champion leaving an account = risk + retention play; new exec arriving from a company where you have installs = expansion play (Clay, Landbase, HockeyStack).
- Freshness cadence: strategic accounts weekly, active pipeline monthly, full database quarterly (Derrick, consistent with HG Insights shelf-life warning).
- Traditional ABM optimizes for planning cadence; data-driven ABM optimizes for market signal (HG Insights).

## Decision rules
- IF account intent spikes but fit score is low THEN do not activate; add to watch list (noise without fit).
- IF intent signal is 0–7 days old THEN route to immediate SDR action; 8–30 days → nurture touch; >30 days → suppress.
- IF a champion leaves a target account THEN trigger retention/relationship play within 7 days (Clay use case).
- IF account adopts a new technology in your category's complement space THEN treat as buying signal + alert (Landbase "New Tech Adopted").
- IF account list is older than one quarter THEN re-score before any campaign spend (HG Insights).
- IF evaluating intent vendors THEN treat all lift/accuracy claims as T3 vendor benchmarks; test on your own closed-won data (Amplemarket's comparison rates even ZoomInfo intent at 12/30 — T3).

## Failure modes
- Static lists; campaigns on calendar cadence instead of account behavior.
- Buying intent data without fit scoring (noise).
- Acting on intent spikes without investigating context (a spike can be an analyst researching for a blog post).
- Data enrichment without refresh — installs change as contracts renew.
