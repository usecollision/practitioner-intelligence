## Beliefs
- Search volume is the most abused metric in SEO: taking it at face value leads to wrong marketing decisions and wasted resources (OPINION — his repeated theme).
- Higher search volume ≠ more traffic: rankings deliver traffic across many keyword variations, not just the one keyword (FRAMEWORK).

## Frameworks
- **Traffic Potential**: for any keyword, look at the top-ranking page's TOTAL organic traffic from all keywords it ranks for — that's the realistic prize, not the keyword's volume (FRAMEWORK — Ahrefs metric he championed).
- **Volume un-grouping**: cluster volumes (e.g., Google Keyword Planner grouping) hide individual keyword reality; mixing clickstream data un-groups them (FRAMEWORK).

## Processes
1. Never trust a single keyword's volume as the traffic estimate.
2. Examine top-ranking pages and their total traffic footprint.
3. Use Traffic Potential to rank opportunities.
4. Choose topics whose top pages actually earn traffic (not just volume).

## Heuristics
- If the #1 result for a keyword gets little traffic, the keyword is a trap (HEURISTIC).
- Volume estimates are estimations — treat them as ranges, not facts (HEURISTIC).

## Tactics
- Use Traffic Potential as the primary keyword-selection filter (TACTIC).
- Challenge conventional marketing knowledge with data studies (his signature move) (TACTIC).

## Tools
- Ahrefs Keywords Explorer (Traffic Potential metric).

## Inputs
- Keyword lists, top-page traffic estimates, clickstream-derived volumes.

## Outputs
- Keyword prioritization frameworks; data studies.

## Metrics
- Traffic Potential, actual top-page traffic, keyword-to-traffic conversion.

## Decision rules
- High volume + low Traffic Potential → skip (DECISION RULE).
- Moderate volume + high Traffic Potential (top page earns lots of related-keyword traffic) → strong candidate (DECISION RULE).
- Cluster volumes hiding distinct keywords → research un-grouped volumes before committing content (DECISION RULE).

## Failure modes
- Optimizing for volume instead of traffic potential — the named mistake (warned).
- Building content for keywords whose ranking pages earn nothing (warned).

## Contrarian beliefs
- The industry's most-used keyword metric is systematically misleading (OPINION — backed by Ahrefs data).

## Conditions
- Applies wherever keyword research happens; Traffic Potential needs Ahrefs-class data (small-budget teams approximate with manual top-page traffic checks).

## Limitations
- Traffic Potential is still an estimate; doesn't capture conversion value (needs pairing with Sam Dunning-style bottom-funnel filtering); as a vendor insider he naturally centers Ahrefs tooling.
