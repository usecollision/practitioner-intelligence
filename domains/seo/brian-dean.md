## Beliefs
- The Skyscraper Technique (find the best content for a keyword, make something substantially better, then build links to it) was the defining link/content method of the 2015-2020 era (FRAMEWORK — legacy).
- His evolution: Skyscraper 2.0 moved user intent to the front — "Figure out User Intent → Satisfy User Intent → Optimize for UX signals" — a documented shift from link-first to intent-first (FRAMEWORK, 2018 case study: +652% organic traffic in 7 days, #11 → #5 for a target keyword).

## Frameworks
- **Skyscraper Technique 1.0**: find top-ranking content; create something 10x better; outreach to the linkers (FRAMEWORK — legacy).
- **Skyscraper Technique 2.0**: intent-first — identify user intent, satisfy it better than anyone, then optimize UX signals (FRAMEWORK).

## Processes (2.0)
1. Determine user intent for the target keyword.
2. Build/rebuild the page to satisfy intent better than current top results.
3. Optimize UX signals (layout, readability, engagement).
4. (1.0 layer) Outreach to sites linking to the weaker existing content.

## Heuristics
- "The original Skyscraper Technique still works GREAT" — but only when combined with intent satisfaction (HEURISTIC).
- Rank improvements follow intent satisfaction, not link volume alone (HEURISTIC).

## Tactics
- Content upgrades (10x better than the incumbent), listicles, data assets; outreach with a specific improvement rationale (TACTIC).

## Tools
- Ahrefs-class link research, Google Search Console.

## Inputs
- Top-10 SERP analysis, linking-page inventories for target keywords.

## Outputs
- Content/outreach briefs; case studies.

## Metrics
- Rankings, organic traffic, referring domains.

## Decision rules
- Incumbent content is weak (thin, dated, poorly structured) + you can fund 10x production → Skyscraper play (DECISION RULE).
- SERP intent is product-led → Skyscraper-style blog content fails; build the asset the SERP wants instead (DECISION RULE — bridge to Eli Schwartz's product-led view).

## Failure modes
- The technique degrades when everyone uses it: 10x became "1.1x", outreach inbox saturation — the reason it's a legacy play (OPINION/EMPIRICAL field consensus).
- Building "better" content without intent analysis (the 1.0 failure mode he corrected in 2.0).

## Contrarian beliefs (era-specific)
- He pre-dated and then adopted the intent-first consensus; his lasting contribution is that link building must be paired with superior on-page assets (OPINION).

## Conditions
- Worked best in informational niches with weak incumbent content and motivated linkers; era: pre-2022. Modern conditions: works only with genuine differentiation and at smaller scale.

## Limitations
- 10x content production costs are high and returns have compressed; outreach fatigue; Google's link valuation changed (SpamBrain); his methods are now baseline knowledge, not edge (classic status).
