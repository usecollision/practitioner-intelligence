## Beliefs
- The daily record of Google's changes is itself a decision input: SEOs who don't track unconfirmed updates, volatility, and platform changes are flying blind (OPINION — his editorial stance).
- Distinguishing confirmed vs unconfirmed updates matters: most volatility events are unconfirmed and SEOs should treat them as signals, not verdicts (FRAMEWORK — his reporting convention).

## Frameworks
- **Confirmed/unconfirmed update taxonomy**: reports "unconfirmed update" for volatility without Google confirmation; tracks rollout timelines (FRAMEWORK).
- **Weekly volatility reporting**: continuous tracking of ranking volatility as the field's canary (FRAMEWORK).

## Processes
1. Monitor volatility trackers (Semrush/Moz-style tools) + industry reports.
2. Correlate with known/rumored update dates.
3. Report confirmed (Google-announced) vs unconfirmed separately.
4. Aggregate the week's search news (SERPs, GSC features, crawler behavior, Ads).

## Heuristics
- A volatility spike without confirmation is worth watching, not reacting to (HEURISTIC).
- Platform behavior changes (e.g., crawler HTTP methods, GSC report changes) matter as much as algorithm updates (HEURISTIC — his weekly video covers both).
