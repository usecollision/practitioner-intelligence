## Beliefs
- A combination of content marketing and digital PR is the best way to build top-tier links — the belief her agency kept unchanged through 40,000+ links built for clients since 2012 (OPINION/EMPIRICAL).
- "If you want major publications to link back to your site, you have to provide them with something that's interesting, accurate, relevant to their audience — and new" (PRINCIPLE).
- Original research is the highest-performing linkable asset type — and as more people pitch writers, the bar keeps rising (EMPIRICAL).

## Frameworks
- **Original research methodology for link acquisition**: credible methodology → data journalists can use → coverage → links (FRAMEWORK).
- **Internal data first**: many brands sit on fascinating proprietary data that can be published — check internal data before commissioning new studies (FRAMEWORK/HEURISTIC).

## Processes
1. Inventory internal data that could interest the audience/public (often overlooked).
2. Design a credible methodology (sample, analysis, caveats) so journalists trust it.
3. Create a shareable asset (study, report, interactive, survey) matched to media beats.
4. Pitch to writers with the story angle, not the link.
5. Measure links and adapt (idea generation, pitching, reporting all iterated over time).

## Heuristics
- Journalists link to data they can cite with confidence — methodology credibility is a link prerequisite (HEURISTIC).
- Survey/report fatigue is real: novelty + rigor beat volume (HEURISTIC).

## Tactics
- Run recurring data studies (annual surveys, industry reports) that become predictable link assets (TACTIC).
- Design assets for embeddability/citability (charts, stats, quotable numbers) (TACTIC).

## Tools
- Survey platforms, data analysis, media/press databases (specifics UNKNOWN).

## Inputs
- Internal data access, methodology design, media landscape knowledge.

## Outputs
- Research assets, data stories, link reports.

## Metrics
- Referring domains, link quality (publication authority), coverage volume.

## Decision rules
- Internal data exists and is publishable → build the study before commissioning research (DECISION RULE).
- No credible methodology possible → don't run the study; credibility loss damages future pitches (DECISION RULE).
- Pitch with a story/angle, not a link request → otherwise treat as wasted outreach (DECISION RULE).

## Failure modes
- Publishing studies with weak methodologies — journalists and the industry call them out (warned).
- Pitching without a news angle as the space saturates (EMPIRICAL — her noted hardening of the field).

## Contrarian beliefs
- Link building and content marketing should not be separate functions — the content IS the link strategy (OPINION, an organizational-structure stance).

## Conditions
- Works for brands with data (SaaS, e-commerce, B2B with usage data, media); needs a content production budget; strongest in data-rich verticals (finance, tech, consumer).

## Limitations
- Expensive and slow; results lumpy (campaign-dependent); hard to replicate without genuine data assets; local/SMB contexts can't usually play this game.
