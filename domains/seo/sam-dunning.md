## Beliefs
- B2B SEO should feed the sales team demos — traffic without demos is failure (OPINION — his core stance).
- The website should be a "24/7 sales rep": what you do, how you help, offer in action, pricing, proof, FAQs, easy booking (FRAMEWORK).
- AI search (ChatGPT) pulls from Google rankings — winning classic SERPs + citable structure is the 2026 B2B play (OPINION/TACTIC: "ChatGPT pulls from Google").

## Frameworks
- **The 90-day "boring" SaaS AI SEO strategy (7 steps)**: blueprint → moat (website) → demo-cooking pages → past media → control the narrative (FRAMEWORK, from his 2026 LinkedIn post).
- **Bottom-funnel exhaustion**: dominate every query where prospects are in buying mode — "[competitor] alternative", "[competitor] pricing", "[competitor] vs [you]", "best [tool] for [niche]" (FRAMEWORK).

## Processes
1. Map every way a prospect searches when in buying mode (alternatives, comparisons, use cases, integrations).
2. Use Ahrefs to find bottom-funnel pages already ranking but not #1 — prioritize updates, linkbuilding, and brand mentions on those "gems".
3. Build net-new bottom-funnel pages; study what ranks in Google and what gets cited in AI answers; "blow it out the water" with customer research, unique stats, product visuals, proof points, answers to tough sales-call questions; answer up front (LLMs pull from it).
4. Build own "best X software" listicles with fair comparisons (where you're a good AND poor fit) to control the narrative.
5. Pitch external "best X" article authors with value trades (placement on your listicle, podcast slot, service exchange, shoutout — "some are pay to play").
6. Mine past media: find execs' press/podcast coverage; contact sites citing them that didn't link; get links/mentions added.
7. Ship pages fast: "take months to go from keyword idea to published page" is a listed mistake (TACTIC — velocity matters).

## Heuristics
- Pages already ranking but not #1 are the fastest wins — cheaper than net-new (HEURISTIC).
- AI answers reward up-front summaries and unique data — structure pages for extraction (HEURISTIC).
- JTBD/problem-aware content ("how to fix [expensive problem]") positions your offer as the new way (HEURISTIC).

## Tactics
- Competitor-alternative and comparison pages as a named category (TACTIC).
- Value-trade outreach to listicle authors (TACTIC).
- Reclaim unlinked brand mentions from past media (TACTIC).
- Weave customer research into every page (TACTIC).

## Tools
- Ahrefs, LinkedIn Sales Navigator-class research, ChatGPT (as a research/citation target).

## Inputs
- Sales call transcripts, customer research, competitor SERP/AI citations, existing ranking data.

## Outputs
- Bottom-funnel page plans, comparison content, AI-citation-targeted pages.

## Metrics
- Demos/sales calls attributed to organic; bottom-funnel keyword coverage; AI citations.

## Decision rules
- Page ranking 3-10 for a bottom-funnel term → update + build links to it before creating new content (DECISION RULE).
- Prospect is comparing vendors → build "[competitor] alternative/vs" pages with honest fit framing (DECISION RULE).
- High-volume informational keyword that won't produce demos → skip (DECISION RULE — his explicit mistake list).
- AI answers cite competitors for your terms → restructure page with up-front answers + unique data (DECISION RULE).

## Failure modes (his explicit list)
- High-volume keywords driving traffic but zero demos (EMPIRICAL — named as weekly-observed mistake).
- No customer research in content → can't convert high-ACV deals (EMPIRICAL).
- Matching competitor pages instead of exceeding them (EMPIRICAL).
- Avoiding "[competitor] alternative" keywords out of fear (EMPIRICAL).
- Slow keyword-to-publish cycles (months) (EMPIRICAL).
- Missing JTBD content for problem-aware prospects (EMPIRICAL).

## Contrarian beliefs
- Competitor-comparison content is an offensive weapon, not a risk (OPINION — many brands avoid it).
- Traffic volume is a vanity metric for B2B; demo count is the metric (OPINION).

## Conditions
- B2B/SaaS with a sales team that takes demos; markets with visible competitor ecosystems; works with modest content budgets if velocity is maintained.

## Limitations
- Less relevant to e-commerce, local, or PLG-self-serve (no demos to feed); comparison-page play needs genuine product differences; AI-search advice is fast-moving (2026-era, may date).
