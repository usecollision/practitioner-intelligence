## Beliefs
- GTM is empirical: study what 30+ companies actually did, not theory (EMPIRICAL).
- First customers come from non-scalable channels — "none of these strategies scale. That's why they work" (HEURISTIC, T1).
## Principles
- B2B early GTM sequence: network → strategic cold outbound → investor networks → communities (add value first) → content (T1).
- Cold outbound works if creative; PR rarely does; content-following founders are surprisingly common (T1).
- Trust is the key currency for first customers (T1).
## Frameworks
- GTM motions taxonomy: PLG vs sales-led vs partner-led; mapped across 30 B2B SaaS companies (FRAMEWORK, T1).
- GTM motion must match product complexity + ACV + buyer (EMPIRICAL, T2).
## Decision rules
- IF ACV high + complex sale THEN sales-led motion with marketing as demand creation + enablement (T2).
- IF low ACV + self-serve THEN PLG (T2).
- IF first 10 customers still missing THEN founders do it personally; don't hire a sales team yet (T1).
- IF joining communities THEN contribute value first, pitch never (T1).
## Failure modes
- Hiring enterprise AE team before PMF/early revenue (T2).
- Picking a GTM motion from fashion rather than product reality (T2).
- Expecting scale from day one; early GTM is hand-to-hand (T1).
## Conditions
- B2B SaaS, any stage; content-driven founder-led motion works best pre-seed→Series A (T1).
