## Beliefs
- Most B2B marketing doesn't create demand because attribution models & KPIs don't incentivize it — "it's really that simple" (OPINION, T1).
- Capture-only marketing rides the coattails of existing category demand; by definition you're not the category leader (OPINION, T1).
- "Losers compete for demand. Winners CREATE demand." (OPINION, T1).
## Principles
- Win higher in the funnel: market to the ~99% not actively buying (T1).
- If you wait for "in-market" or "intent data," you've already lost (T1).
- Never value attribution over effectiveness (T1).
## Frameworks
- Demand capture vs demand creation dichotomy (FRAMEWORK, T1).
- Attribution blind spots: WOM, podcast influence, share-with-boss — models mis-measure them (T1).
## Heuristics
- Capture channels hit diminishing returns fast; scaling them far beyond returns = $1M/mo Google ads with 36-month CAC payback (example, T1).
- In 4-vendor deals at the bottom of the funnel, you're not the brand of choice (T1).
## Decision rules
- IF attribution only rewards capture THEN add awareness/brand KPIs before scaling capture (T1).
- IF budget is tiny and every dollar must convert this quarter THEN capture is defensible — creation is a funded strategy (condition, T2).
- IF competitors are creating demand and you're not THEN they will steal share (T1).
## Failure modes
- Marketing org optimized to last-click → no demand creation at all (T1).
- Scaling capture beyond diminishing returns (T1).
## Conditions
- B2B, funded ($2M+ ARR or VC), category with competitors; fails for survival-stage budgets (T2).
