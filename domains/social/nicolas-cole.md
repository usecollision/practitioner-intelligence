- ## Beliefs — Online writing is a skill of volume + frameworks, not talent; succeed on ONE platform first (his arc: Quora 2014 → Twitter primary today); LinkedIn = "Timeless Resource Library of Content" for new connections to audit, not a distribution launchpad.
- ## Principles
  - Every idea can be written 4 ways (4A Framework): Actionable (here's how), Analytical (here's a breakdown), Aspirational (yes, you can), Anthropological (here's why).
  - Republish everywhere: same atomic essay (~300 words) cross-posted; primary platform + secondary libraries.
  - Your profile content is a sales asset: new connections read your library and DM you.
- ## Frameworks — 4A Framework; Atomic Essay (~300 words); Ultimate Online Writing Republishing Framework; Ghostwriting templates (e.g., "The frameworks" template).
- ## Processes — daily writing habit (Ship 30 for 30 cohort model) → publish atomic essay → republish to 3-5 platforms → let profile library compound.
- ## Heuristics — 300-word essays; one idea per essay; write inside a template that shows length; beginner focus = one platform.
- ## Metrics — library depth (articles posted), inbound DMs from strangers, platform-specific engagement; distribution ≠ primary goal on LinkedIn.
- ## Decision rules
  - IF starting out THEN commit to one platform for 6+ months before adding others. (HEURISTIC, T2)
  - IF stuck for ideas THEN run the same idea through 4A lenses. (FRAMEWORK, T2)
  - IF goal = inbound leads from LinkedIn THEN publish articles/newsletters (they stay on profile) rather than chasing feed reach. (HEURISTIC, T2)
  - IF you've succeeded on one platform THEN republish, don't create fresh per platform. (HEURISTIC, T2)
- ## Failure modes — beginners splitting across platforms (mastery on none); treating LinkedIn as a distribution launchpad; writing without frameworks (blank page).
- ## Conditions — works for individual writers/ghostwriters building personal brands; volume habit required (daily writing).
- ## Limitations — his model sells writing education; raw "volume" advice without frameworks underdelivers; LinkedIn reach minimalism conflicts with vDB's 2026 data (video push, long-form still strong — actually aligned: he favors long-form/articles).
