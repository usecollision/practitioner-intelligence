- ## Beliefs — LinkedIn rebuilt its algorithm (Relationship Graph → Interest Graph, model "360 Brew"); reach is now 50% baseline norm + 29.5% post performance + 20.5% luck. Follower count no longer guarantees reach (followers +31%, impressions fell, 1.3M-post dataset).
- ## Principles
  - The algorithm rewards posts abnormally good FOR YOU (4x your norm → +639.6% reach), not objectively good posts.
  - Distribution is per-format; format choice = which job the post does (in-network vs out-of-network).
  - Performance signals: comment-thread depth, saves, native DM-sends (tested: 800 posts → ~60% reach uptick) — likes are weak.
- ## Frameworks — Interest Graph distribution model; reach equation (baseline 50% / performance 29.5% / luck 20.5%); format job matrix.
- ## Processes — annual Algorithm Insights Report (1.3M posts, 12,000+ creators tracked); seasonal baseline adjustment (summer dips 18-40% are seasonal, not strategic).
- ## Heuristics — top 5% posts: 39,061 median impressions; bottom 80%: 629. No-hashtag posts +5-10%. Documents 1.39x. Polls 1.78x reach / 0.37 engagement. Lo-fi video 1.8-2.6x. Carousels -40% reach (2026). Text <600 chars underperforms. 3+ links +20% vs 1.
- ## Metrics — reach vs own norm, in-network vs out-of-network split, saves, reposts, DM-sends, comment-thread replies, engagement rate.
- ## Decision rules
  - IF goal = new eyes THEN images/long-form/articles (47-52% out-of-network); IF deepening existing audience THEN polls/reposts/live (72-76% in-network). (EMPIRICAL, T1)
  - IF second consecutive text post THEN rotate format (-25% reach on #2). (EMPIRICAL, T1)
  - IF people ghost replies to your comments THEN expect -35% comment reach; always follow up. (EMPIRICAL, T1)
  - IF you delete posts THEN stop: signals instability. (EMPIRICAL, T1)
  - IF connections don't post/engage in your topic THEN prune: they tank distribution. (EMPIRICAL, T1)
  - IF content is AI-slop THEN expect suppression (AI detection + "Celebrate an Occasion" boost shows network rebalancing). (EMPIRICAL/T2)
- ## Failure modes — engagement pods (detected 97% accuracy), DM spam (low response rates suppress profile), comment-and-run, hashtag decoration, gating content (free = 20-25x more reach), connection farming.
- ## Contrarian beliefs — "never post links" is half-true (3+ links outperform 1); carousels are dying; follower count can hurt you.
- ## Conditions — data is from personal profiles mostly; company pages differ (impressions +33%, engagement -6.2%); findings are 2026-specific and shift quarterly.
- ## Limitations — single researcher selling the report (self-promotional framing); sample skews to creators/coaches; cannot independently verify his dataset. Treat numbers as directional.
