- ## Beliefs — LinkedIn reach is a system you operate, not content you gamble; distribution discipline beats writing talent. 900M+ impressions over 5 years powering consulting/product/subscription/sponsorship revenue.
- ## Principles
  - Produce every day from a curated idea bank, not inspiration (ContentOS: Curate → Templatize → Rapidly create → Distribute + Boost; 20-30 posts/day).
  - One pillar idea → 7 formats (story, listicle, teardown, observation, contrarian take, past vs present, future prediction).
  - "Meat first, trailer second": write the teaching core, then the hook that sells the click.
- ## Frameworks
  - ContentOS (4-stage daily production system)
  - 1-7 content multiplication (pillar → 7 posts)
  - Meat/Trailer (content body vs hook)
- ## Processes — daily: curate ideas → templatize into formats → batch create → distribute across channels + boost winners with paid when ROI clears.
- ## Heuristics — if you can't write 20 posts from your idea bank, you don't have a system; if a post overperforms, repurpose it within 48h.
- ## Metrics — impressions (baseline), engagement rate, follower growth, revenue per content stream; he publishes his own numbers (162M→900M impressions).
- ## Decision rules
  - IF no idea bank of 50+ curated items THEN build that before posting daily (system before output). (HEURISTIC, T2)
  - IF a piece resonates 4x+ your norm THEN boost it with paid / repurpose into 7 formats. (HEURISTIC, T2)
  - IF you have a product to sell THEN route content to revenue streams (consulting/product/subscription/sponsorship), not just reach. (HEURISTIC, T2)
  - IF time is limited THEN one platform with a system beats five platforms without one. (HEURISTIC, T2)
- ## Failure modes — posting without an idea bank (burnout, inconsistency); writing "trailers" without meat (no follow-through value); gating content too early.
- ## Conditions — works for solo operators/B2B personal brands with 6-12 months runway; depends on consistent daily output.
- ## Limitations — his model monetizes attention (courses, sponsorships); a company account needs different mechanics (vDB: employee posts engage 2x brand posts).
