- ## Beliefs — Storytelling is the core skill of audience building; structure and purpose precede craft; "draft fast, edit slow" defeats blank-page anxiety.
- ## Principles — Clarity of purpose (what should the story achieve?); define the audience before writing; establish structure (story spine: Kenn Adams' "and but therefore"); weave emotion; infuse novelty; create contrasts (Nancy Duarte's "what is vs what could be"); keep it simple; make it shareable; draft fast, edit slow.
- ## Frameworks — Story Spine (Kenn Adams); What Is vs What Could Be (Duarte); Curiosity-gap narrative structures in his newsletters.
- ## Processes — define purpose → define audience → structure → draft fast → edit slow → publish → iterate.
- ## Heuristics — if you can't state the purpose, the audience feels it; shareable = one clear emotion + one clear idea.
- ## Metrics — newsletter growth, reply rate, shares; he treats storytelling as the input to audience trust, not vanity reach.
- ## Decision rules
  - IF a piece has no clear purpose THEN don't publish — audiences feed on commitment and clarity. (HEURISTIC, T2)
  - IF blank-page stuck THEN draft garbage fast, edit slow. (HEURISTIC, T2)
  - IF a story has no contrast ("what is vs what could be") THEN add one before publishing. (FRAMEWORK, T2)
  - IF goal = shares THEN keep one emotion + one idea per piece. (HEURISTIC, T2)
- ## Failure modes — stories without structure (flimsy foundation); writing for everyone (no audience); polishing while drafting (never finishing).
- ## Conditions — personal-brand audience building; applies to X threads, newsletters, LinkedIn.
- ## Limitations — T2 at best; his growth claims (multi-million audiences) unverified; principles are craft-level, not platform-algorithm-level.
