- ## Beliefs — Twitter/X is algorithm-based: certain writing styles systematically win; study viral creators and swipe their mechanics (he openly swiped hooks from Joey Justice and Dan Koe). Virality is engineered, not luck.
- ## Principles
  - Rule of One: one big idea, one story, one emotion, one benefit, one CTA per thread.
  - Hook with PAS (Problem-Agitation-Solution); open by calling out the target group ("Do you sit at a computer for that long?").
  - Value beats length: 50% Rule — cut first draft in half. Clarity over cleverness.
  - "The worst thing you can be online is boring."
- ## Frameworks — PAS hook; Rule of One; Zombie Test (kill passive voice); Slippery Slope writing (curiosity/questions/open loops); sticky packaging (juxtaposition, alliteration, symmetry, rhyme, rhythm).
- ## Processes — teardown his own winning posts (156k likes/20M impressions on one thread); build a swipe file of 1,400+ saved tweets; write line-by-line; round-2 rewrite your best ideas.
- ## Heuristics — pictures average +35% boost in retweets; 2nd tweet of a thread is nearly as important as the first; sentence final words land hardest (finish strong); broad problems = broader potential.
- ## Metrics — impressions, likes, retweets, follower growth per post; he tracks performance by format/hook pattern, not just totals.
- ## Decision rules
  - IF opening line doesn't call out a specific reader THEN rewrite it (PAS). (HEURISTIC, T2)
  - IF thread lacks a picture THEN add one (+35% RTs). (EMPIRICAL as reported, T2)
  - IF draft > 2x target length THEN cut 50% before publishing. (HEURISTIC, T2)
  - IF you're not seeing traction THEN study 3 viral creators in your niche and remix their structures — don't invent from scratch. (HEURISTIC, T2)
  - IF a post overperforms THEN rewrite it round 2 with a better hook. (HEURISTIC, T2)
- ## Failure modes — trying to say too much (no Rule of One); holding back emotion ("twist the knife"); publishing without a swipe file; big promises without delivery ("or no RTs").
- ## Conditions — works for individual creators writing daily; X-native; the mechanics transfer to LinkedIn/threads formats.
- ## Limitations — survivorship bias in his own wins (he sells a swipe-file product); 2022-era examples may not match 2026 X algorithm; follower-count claims not reliable.
