# SYNTHESIS — Paid Strategy & Media Planning

Practitioners: Les Binet & Peter Field (researchers, IPA databank), Byron Sharp (researcher, Ehrenberg-Bass), Mark Ritson (educator, two-speed planning), Eric Seufert (analyst, incrementality/MMM), + Chris Walker/Refine Labs (B2B budget allocation, supplementary). Verified 2026-08-14.

## Consensus
- **Brand and activation are complementary, not substitutes** — "brand sets up the sale, activation closes the sale" (Ritson); efficiency gains from activation require brand (Binet & Field); performance alone cannot create demand that does not already exist (Walker/Francois). Binet & Field, Sharp, Ritson, and Walker all independently identify **under-investment in brand as the dominant paid-media failure** (EMPIRICAL for Binet/Sharp; OPINION/EMPIRICAL for Walker).
- **Reach beats targeting for the brand job**: whole-market/category-buyer reach grows brands; loyalty-targeted or existing-customer campaigns underperform (Binet & Field: 3x large effects; Sharp: penetration is the growth lever; Ritson: don't target customers who'd buy anyway). EMPIRICAL (Binet, Sharp).
- **Platform attribution overstates performance**: last-click/self-attributed ROAS inflates channel contribution; macro-level metrics (MER/MMM/incrementality) are the truth layer (Seufert; AdMaxxer/AdSights topic evidence: overlap tax 1.4–1.8x; iROAS on brand search ~10–25% of reported). EMPIRICAL/OPINION (multiple independent sources).
- **Different metrics for different decisions**: short-term/activation measured with ROI; long-term/brand with brand effects (Binet & Field principle 10; Ritson: "stop measuring brand with dollar estimates"; Seufert: MMM macro + campaign micro on separate cadences). FRAMEWORK.
- **Holding a split for years beats quarterly rebalancing on ROI swings** (Ritson: hold ~10 years; Francois: review annually, not quarterly; Binet: long-term effects only visible over 3+ years). HEURISTIC/EMPIRICAL.
- **Creativity + consistency compound**: emotional, distinctive-asset-consistent creative outperforms rational benefit-led persuasion (Binet: emotional 2x; Sharp: DBAs + memory refresh). EMPIRICAL.

## Disagreement
1. **Is 60/40 universal?** Binet & Field themselves say NO (aggregate sweet spot now 62:38; sector range wide; IPA blog: "no universal best practice ratio"). Growth-stage practitioners (Francois, Stackmatix) say 60/40 is "roughly wrong" for early stage — closer to 30/70–20/80. Resolution: the ratio is context-dependent; the *direction* of adjustment is counterintuitive and evidence-based (high-consideration → more brand), while stage-based adjustments are practitioner heuristic.
2. **Double-duty vs two-speed executions** — Ritson (Zealot) argues separate brand and activation campaigns; the Double-Duty Squad argues one execution can do both (cheaper, attention is scarce). Ritson cites Binet/Field data favoring two-speed; Binet/Field themselves note double-duty can work. Condition: budget size and creative quality.
3. **Can brand ROI be measured?** Ritson: stop trying (ridiculous dollar estimates). Seufert: econometric measurement (MMM/incrementality) is exactly how you measure the un-attributable. Condition: data volume + modeling capability; both agree last-click can't do it.
4. **Reach vs efficiency under small budgets** — Sharp: even with limited budgets, don't think small/low-reach (not a recipe for growth or maintenance). Lean-team practitioners (Spike): concentrate spend above minimum viable thresholds, possibly 90/10 on one channel — which for a tiny budget means almost no brand reach. Condition: budget size; below a threshold the brand job shifts to cheap brand assets (founder narrative, content, distinctiveness) rather than paid reach (Stackmatix; Walker).
5. **B2B specifics** — Binet/Field data: B2B optimum ≈ 46% brand / 54% activation. Walker/Refine Labs: Brand 20–30% / Demand 50–60% / Expand 10–20% as a working B2B model. Condition: sales-cycle length, whether the sales team covers the activation job, and whether "brand" includes founder-led organic content (which Walker counts as brand at low cost).

## Conditions
- **62:38-ish brand:activation applies when**: established brand, validated channels, 3+ year payback tolerance, B2C, high-consideration/rational categories (need MORE brand), online-first businesses (need MORE brand), financial services-like sectors (EMPIRICAL, Binet & Field).
- **Performance-heavy split (70–80% activation) applies when**: pre-PMF/early stage, channel economics unvalidated, cash-constrained with short payback window, <12–18 months runway, no brand measurement infrastructure (HEURISTIC — Francois, Stackmatix; consistent with Seufert's payback-window rule).
- **Reach-maximizing brand media applies when**: category exists, budget above the minimum viable threshold for the medium, brand can survive a delayed payback (EMPIRICAL, Sharp/Binet).
- **Incrementality/MMM layer applies when**: multi-channel spend, >~$50–100k/month paid (data volume), data capability exists; below that, MER + platform lift tests suffice (OPINION — synthesis inference from Seufert's dual-workflow + AdSights sample-size guidance).
- **Two-speed (not double-duty) applies when**: budget supports both streams; otherwise run integrated campaigns but keep separate *measurement* of long and short effects (FRAMEWORK — Ritson/Binet).

## Evidence evaluation
- **EMPIRICAL (strongest)**: Binet & Field IPA databank analyses (62:38 aggregate; sector variance; whole-market 3x; emotional 2x; long-term 2x profit; online brands below optimum at 56%). Sharp's Ehrenberg-Bass laws (double jeopardy, penetration growth, mental availability ≠ awareness). Seufert's structural arguments on overlap (multi-channel redundancy is near-certain; directionally uncontested).
- **EMPIRICAL but tool-vendor-sourced**: MER vs ROAS overlap-tax numbers (1.4–1.8x), iROAS benchmarks (brand search 0.10–0.25 incrementality factor; retargeting 0.20–0.35), sample sizes (5–15k users/arm) — from AdMaxxer/AdSights/Metricuno guides; credible, consistent across vendors, but vendors sell measurement tools (tier 3, treat as directional).
- **HEURISTIC**: stage-based brand ratios (Stackmatix table, Francois 25/75–40/60), Spike's 60-70/20-30/10 lean split, kill criteria, Ritson's 10%-of-turnover.
- **OPINION**: Ritson's anti-brand-ROI stance; Walker's "losers compete for demand"; Seufert's "retire LTV" framing.
- **Gaps**: no RCT-level evidence for 60/40 (IPA databank is correlational, award-entry-biased — UNVERIFIED critique, widely discussed); little public evidence for MMM vs MER accuracy at small budgets; Binet's online-brand findings pre-date full AI-bidding era.

## Outliers (worth investigating)
- **"Rational categories need MORE brand"** — the single most counterintuitive, evidence-backed finding (Binet & Field 2022). Directly contradicts intuition-driven allocation; should be a hard-coded OS rule.
- **Overlap tax as a management metric** — sum of platform ROAS × spend vs total revenue; >35% gap = platform ROAS is fiction (AdMaxxer). Cheap diagnostic for the "attribution-driven misallocation" failure.
- **MER target formula** — target MER ≈ 1.3 / contribution margin (AdMaxxer) as a P&L-level budget health rule.
- **Ghost ads as the cleanest incrementality method** — auction-level randomization removes geo-matching confounds (Garrett Johnson via Seufert; Metricuno).
- **Walker's revenue-source gut check** — map last 2 quarters of inbound revenue to real source via self-reported attribution + sales notes; compare to budget split ("your budget tells the truth"). Cheap, high-value annual audit.

## Failure knowledge (what repeatedly doesn't work)
- **Over-investing in bottom-funnel/demand capture**: scaling capture channels far beyond diminishing returns — e.g., "$1M/month on Google Ads with 36-month CAC payback" (Walker); redundant multi-channel spend guaranteed once >1 channel (Seufert); short-termism "destroying value" (Binet & Field). Sources: Walker 2021; Seufert 2020; Binet & Field 2022.
- **Under-investing in awareness/brand**: online brands running 56% brand vs 62% optimum (Binet & Field); the 12-month ROI trap (Ritson); demand-capture-only B2B teams riding others' category evangelism (Walker).
- **Attribution-driven misallocation**: last-click veneer (Seufert); platform ROAS inflation from overlap (AdMaxxer: 1.4–1.8x; brand-search iROAS ~0.2x reported; Metricuno: brand search reports 10x+, true iROAS 1.5–3x); creative A/B tests mistaken for incrementality (AdSights).
- **Underfunded tests / spreading budget thin**: "underfunded tests are the #1 reason channel strategies fail" (Spike); underpowered incrementality tests produce inconclusive CIs (AdSights).
- **Loyalty/retention-heavy growth strategy**: no empirical justification (Binet & Field; Sharp's penetration law).
- **Fixed splits never revisited**: "fixed splits applied to changing businesses is how marketing investment quietly underperforms for years" (Francois).
- **Bad incrementality test design**: gut-feel geo matching, <6–8 matched pairs, no pre-test baseline — "spending €40k on a confounded holdout is worse than not testing" (Metricuno).
- **Measuring brand with ROI estimates**: produces defunding pressure (Ritson); "if you can't defend brand spend with measurement, finance will defund it" (Francois) — the resolution is brand-effect metrics, not ROI gymnastics.

## Collision Method sketch — "Paid Strategy & Media Plan" (what the Marketing OS should encode)

- **Objective**: produce and govern a paid-media strategy: budget size, brand:activation split, channel plan, and measurement/reallocation loop for a given business context.
- **Prerequisites**: stage (pre-seed→scale), category + consideration level, brand age, online/offline mix, channel validation status, measurement infrastructure, cash position/payback tolerance, last-12-months revenue by real source.
- **Inputs**: revenue and spend by channel (MER-able), platform ROAS, segment data (size/value/share), funnel conversion rates, sector benchmark ratio (Binet/Field ready-reckoner style), previous plan results.
- **Diagnosis**: (1) maturity audit (Francois): validated channels? defensible positioning? measurement infra? (2) budget-truth audit (Walker): map inbound revenue to real source vs budget split; (3) overlap tax check (AdMaxxer): sum(platform ROAS×spend)/revenue − 1; (4) penetration vs loyalty gap (Sharp) where brand data exists.
- **Decision tree**:
  1. If pre-PMF / cash-constrained / <12 mo runway → 20–30% brand ceiling, performance-led, payback window set by cash (Seufert; Francois).
  2. Else if established B2C → start 62:38; if high-consideration/rational or online-first → raise brand share; if low-consideration/emotional or travel-like → lower (Binet & Field).
  3. Else if B2B → 46:54 baseline (Binet/Field), or 20–30% brand motion with founder-led organic counted as brand (Walker); demand 50–60%; expand 10–20%.
  4. Then: brand stream targets whole market/category buyers (Sharp), activation stream targets selected segments (Ritson); hold split ~annually, not quarterly.
- **Methodology**: Ritson's plan structure (12-month increment; diagnosis→strategy→tactics; segmentation→targeting arms→positioning→pointy objectives→tactics with costs); Sharp's reach/DBAs for creative; Binet's long/short principles as the ratio engine.
- **Execution**: two-speed streams where budget allows; lean teams: 60–70% primary channel / 20–30% compounding / ≤10% experiments, all above minimum viable spend thresholds (Spike).
- **Metrics**: MER at P&L level (target ≈ 1.3/contribution margin); platform ROAS only for creative/optimization; iROAS for budget decisions; MMM quarterly if scale supports; brand effects (mental availability/CEPs, share of search, branded query volume) for the brand stream.
- **Reallocation loop**: monthly: MER drift (>10% jump = overlap/retargeting problem); quarterly: incrementality tests on suspect channels (brand search, retargeting, PMax — test first per AdSights); annual: split review vs stage (Francois).
- **Stopping rules**: cut channel if iROAS < contribution-margin breakeven after a valid test; kill criteria 3-of-4 (conversion volume, CAC > 2x target, declining marginal returns, team-time drain — Spike); never cut prospecting on reported ROAS alone (AdSights: 1.3x prospecting can feed 4.5x MER); stop brand-measurement-via-ROI debates — use brand metrics.
- **Failure modes (guardrails)**: bottom-funnel over-scaling (Walker/Seufert), brand starvation via 12-month trap (Ritson/Binet), overlap tax >35% (AdMaxxer), underfunded tests (Spike), loyalty-targeted brand plans (Sharp), confounded holdouts (Metricuno), fixed splits (Francois).
- **Conditions**: full method for established multi-channel businesses; scaled-down (MER + platform lift tests, no MMM; cheap brand assets instead of paid reach) for small teams.
- **Limitations**: correlational evidence base for ratios; no public evidence at small-budget scale; B2B ratios are practitioner-consensus, not databank-grade.
- **Confidence**: T1 for consensus layer (Binet/Sharp/Seufert primary sources); T2 for ratio numbers and vendor benchmarks; T3 for stage-by-stage percentage tables.
- **Key sources**: Binet & Field *Effectiveness in Context* (2022) + *Long and the Short of It* (2013); Sharp *How Brands Grow* + Ehrenberg-Bass essays; Ritson Marketing Week columns (2021–2022); Seufert Mobile Dev Memo (2018–2024); AdMaxxer/AdSights MER-vs-ROAS; Metricuno incrementality guide; Refine Labs budget article; Spike lean-team framework.
